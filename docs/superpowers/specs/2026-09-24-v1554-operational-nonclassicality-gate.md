Date: 2026-09-24

## Purpose

v15.53 certified a genuine same-retained-readout/distinct-target obstruction in a frozen finite family, but its two permutation-unitary realizations also admit classical finite-group descriptions. v15.54 asks the next narrower question: does an independently verified obstruction survive when at least one distinguishing target structure must exhibit an operational effect unavailable to the registered classical comparator family?

This is not a derivation of quantum mechanics and not a gravity result. It is a controlled nonclassicality gate on an explicitly frozen finite operational family.

## Scientific question

For a frozen operational realization family M54, retained reduction O, structural target equivalence tau, and independently defined classical comparator class C, does there exist an admissible pair m1,m2 such that:

1. O(m1)=O(m2);
2. tau(m1)!=tau(m2);
3. at least one member has a certified operational witness W;
4. no realization in C reproduces the complete registered operational table relevant to W?

A positive result certifies only that the frozen family contains a retained-indistinguishable target distinction with a registered operational table outside the frozen classical comparator class.

A negative result is only NO_NONCLASSICAL_WITNESS_IN_FROZEN_FAMILY.

## Why noncommutativity alone is insufficient

Noncommuting permutations exist in classical finite groups. Therefore [A,B] != 0, order sensitivity, or a nonabelian operation table alone cannot open the nonclassicality gate.

Noncommutativity may be a prerequisite or diagnostic, but the certification target is an operationally observable incompatibility/interference table that the frozen classical comparator class cannot reproduce.

## Registered operational witness

Before candidate enumeration, freeze a witness functional W on observable probability tables.

The first v15.54 gate will use a minimal interference/incompatibility test with all required contexts explicitly represented. W must compare operational probabilities, not internal matrix labels.

Requirements:

- W is computed solely from registered preparation/context/outcome probabilities.
- The threshold is exact and algebraic/rational where possible.
- Candidate IDs, enumeration order, target class, and post-result information are inaccessible to W.
- Relabeling preparations, outcomes, or contexts through an allowed symmetry must preserve the certification result.
- A noncommuting internal representation with no operational violation does not pass.

## Classical comparator class C

Freeze C before witness search.

C consists of finite classical hidden-state realizations with:

- a finite ontic state set bounded by the frozen contract;
- preparation distributions over ontic states;
- stochastic transformations;
- context-appropriate stochastic response functions;
- convex mixing;
- no negative probabilities;
- no complex phase primitive;
- no candidate-specific selector.

For the deliberately small first family, classical reproducibility must be decided exhaustively or by an exact finite linear-feasibility calculation using rational arithmetic. No heuristic optimizer or floating tolerance may decide the verdict.

The ontic-state bound is part of the frozen scope. Failure to find a comparator under that bound is not a theorem against arbitrarily large hidden-state models unless a separate exact cardinality-compression theorem is proved.

## Classicality adjudication

For every candidate that satisfies W:

1. construct its complete registered operational probability table;
2. enumerate or solve exactly over C;
3. return either CLASSICAL_COMPARATOR_FOUND or NO_COMPARATOR_IN_FROZEN_CLASS;
4. provide a concrete comparator when found, or an exact finite infeasibility certificate/check when absent.

A self-declared nonclassical field is forbidden.

Operational witness data are not automatically retained observables. Otherwise the experiment would trivially distinguish the candidates by inserting the answer into E.
