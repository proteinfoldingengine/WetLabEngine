Why the whole family saves only one coordinate

The frozen operators all commute with Γ = Y ⊗ Y ⊗ Y ⊗ Y. For the fully pinched seed, the computed module is the 255-dimensional hyperplane orthogonal to Γ. Its missing coordinate is conserved but invisible to the target record observables. This is a symmetry of the supplied toy generators—not a newly added physical law.

The two states (I+Γ)/16 and (I−Γ)/16 are distinguishable as full quantum states, yet have the same target predictions under the entire supplied family. The module intentionally does not need to distinguish them.

A prediction vector is not a density matrix

Expectation coordinates on the module reproduce the requested outputs. The orthogonal matrix representative need not be positive. In the full-family control, projecting a positive pure state onto Γ’s orthogonal complement produces minimum eigenvalue −1/16. Therefore this projection is not an admissible physical pruning channel.

A smaller predictive description is not automatically a smaller retained quantum world. The original RAS and RCR assumptions are neither replaced nor derived here.
