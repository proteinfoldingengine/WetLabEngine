## Verdict

`CROSS_DOMAIN_MORPHISM_TYPE_NOT_DERIVED_FROM_TESTED_PRIMITIVE_LATTICE`

The question was moved one level upstream: can the *type* of the missing quantum-operational -> retained morphism be forced from common primitive structure, before choosing any map?

The frozen v15.49-v15.51 sequence already supplies a controlled answer for a substantial finite primitive lattice.

### What is derived

From target-blind **ordered recoverability incidence**, the retained leg is derivable: lineage, dependency, recoverability, composition and refinement map naturally into the retained-recoverability target.

### What is not derived

The same primitive object does not originate a quantum leg. v15.51 then added, singly and in combinations:

- composition with interference;
- convex operational distinguishability;
- noncommutative involutive event composition;
- all three together;
- and finally purification plus local tomography.

Across nine preregistered packages, **zero** supplied all required outputs: carrier representation class, subsystem composition, recovery-capable operations and a typed quantum leg.

The gate explicitly forbade assuming Hilbert space, matrix algebra, qubits, Born rule, density operators, CPTP maps, target dimension or tensor factors, because any of those would partially insert the quantum codomain by hand.

Therefore composition/recovery structure does not currently force a cross-domain Hom-set in this audited lattice.

## Scope

This is not a universal theorem that no deeper primitive can do so. It says the tested first-principles package is insufficient.

The minimal missing content is now localized to at least one genuinely quantum-typing ingredient: a carrier representation class, subsystem composition law, recovery-capable operation class, and a typed relation from primitive events to those operational morphisms.

## Research boundary

A next experiment is scientifically meaningful only if a **new independently motivated primitive** is stated before downstream testing and is capable of supplying one of those missing typed ingredients. Otherwise the cross-domain bridge program should stop here.

No downstream curvature, holonomy, ADM, Einstein, or gravity quality may be used to choose that primitive.
