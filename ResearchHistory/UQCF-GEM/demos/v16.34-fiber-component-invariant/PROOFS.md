# v16.34 — Pre-adjudication reasoning

F1. q=tau retains only minimum cover cardinalities. M retains the identities of all indexed minimum covers and is therefore strictly richer in general.

F2. An invisible incidence deletion preserves tau but may change M by removing some minimum covers while leaving at least one survivor. Therefore M is not automatically constant along invisible edges. G1 must be tested before any attempt to use M as a component invariant.

F3. Essential incidence and participation are functions of M, so if M varies inside a connected component these coarser candidates may nevertheless remain constant; neither direction may be assumed.

F4. To classify move-components, an invariant must be constant along every allowed edge. A signature that separates disconnected components but also changes along an edge is not an invariant of components.

F5. If no candidate A–C is constant and separating, the correct result is that these natural minimum-cover summaries do not classify the disconnected fibers. Do not construct an arbitrary hash of component identity.

No physical interpretation is preregistered.
