## 2. Candidate definition, with every space declared

Let `H_A tensor H_B tensor H_C` and an overlap cover `(AB, BC)` be supplied primitive data, not reconstructed from a desired metric. Let `rho_ABC > 0`, with reduced states obtained by partial trace. Define on `H_ABC`

    D(A:C|B) = log rho_ABC
               - (log rho_AB) tensor I_C
               - I_A tensor (log rho_BC)
               + I_A tensor (log rho_B) tensor I_C.

All logarithms are natural logarithms. Equivalently use `ell_S = log(d_S rho_S)`; the dimension-dependent scalar terms cancel. The definition has no length, elapsed time, pruning rate, graph Laplacian, fitted constant, or downstream curvature input.

Faithfulness is a stated mathematical domain, not a claim that physical states must all be full rank. Rank-deficient states require a separate support-qualified construction. No epsilon regulator is added to force this screen to work.

The output is Hermitian, generally indefinite, and lives in an operator algebra. It is NOT automatically a scalar face amplitude, a positive projective support source, an incidence-chain element, or stress-energy. In particular, replacing it by a trace, norm, exponential or selected matrix entry is a further choice which this screen does not make.

## 3. Structural reasons to study it

**Frame covariance.** Under an independent local unitary `U_A tensor U_B tensor U_C`, each lifted log conjugates, hence so does D. This is covariance, not a preferred local frame.

**Independent-copy additivity.** After the declared regrouping of two independent tripartite systems,

    D(rho tensor sigma) = D(rho) tensor I + I tensor D(sigma).

This follows from the logarithm of a tensor product, not a time-evolution law. It is NOT linearity under mixtures or signed event-amplitude addition: D is nonlinear in rho. It cannot be inserted silently into the old linear source model. A linear-response version would require an explicitly specified reference state, tangent input and derivative, or an openly changed source axiom.

**Conditional chain identity.** For a faithful four-partite state, with all operators embedded on the same space,

    D(A:CD|B) = D(A:C|B) + D(A:D|BC).

The intermediate logarithms cancel exactly. No commutation assumption is needed for this sum identity. It is not an ADM constraint or a curvature/Bianchi theorem.

**Recovery null.** The zero operator is the familiar logarithmic equality condition for strong subadditivity, and the equality states have a quantum Markov/recovery interpretation [R1,R2]. This connects the candidate to recoverability before geometry, but the published theorem does not call it a gravitational source.

For diagonal states, `exp D = p_ABC p_B/(p_AB p_BC)` entrywise. That scalar-ratio expression is used ONLY for commuting test states; it is not substituted for the noncommuting operator formula.

## 4. Exact descent counterexample: lower-order data are insufficient

For a static correlation parameter `r` with `|r|<1`, take

    rho_r = (I_8 + r Z_A tensor Z_B tensor Z_C)/8.

Every one-body marginal is `I_2/2` and every two-body marginal is `I_4/4`, independently of r. Every rho_r is a valid globally compatible state. Yet

    D(rho_r) = log(I_8 + r Z_A tensor Z_B tensor Z_C)
             = (log(1-r^2)/2) I_8
               + (log((1+r)/(1-r))/2) Z_A tensor Z_B tensor Z_C.

At r=0 this vanishes; at r=1/2 it does not. The eigenvalues of rho_(1/2) are 3/16 and 1/16, so the example is well inside the positive cone, not a rank-loss or spectral-edge artifact.

Therefore no function of the pairwise tuple `(rho_AB,rho_AC,rho_BC)` alone can reproduce D for all faithful joint completions. This is a candidate-specific no-descent result, not a theorem that a primitive physical joint state cannot exist.

Supplying the actual joint state resolves this ambiguity. Choosing a convenient completion from the marginal fiber does not: that would introduce a new selection rule, which must be justified independently. No maximum-entropy completion, minimum-norm rule or curvature-dependent selection is adopted here.

The plus/minus r cases are related by a local bit flip; their conjugated operators do NOT establish different gauge-invariant physical source signs. The decisive counterexample is r=0 versus r=1/2, whose global spectra differ despite identical pairwise marginals.

## 5. Noncommuting-overlap check

Let

    P = X_A tensor X_B tensor I_C,
    Q = I_A tensor Z_B tensor Z_C,
    rho = (I_8 + r(P+Q))/8,  r=1/4.

Here `PQ=-QP`, `(P+Q)^2=2I`, and `2r^2=1/8<1`, so rho is strictly positive. Its AB and BC marginal logarithms need not commute when lifted. Exact functional calculus gives

    D = alpha I + gamma(P+Q),
    alpha = log(1-2r^2)/2 - log(1-r^2),
    gamma = atanh(sqrt(2)r)/sqrt(2) - atanh(r).

For `0<r<1/sqrt(2)`,

    gamma = sum_(n>=1) (2^n-1) r^(2n+1)/(2n+1) > 0.

Thus the candidate is nonzero for this noncommuting-overlap family as well. The exact matrix checks establish the anticommutation, marginals and spectral polynomial; the displayed convergent-series argument establishes strict nonvanishing. No floating matrix-log threshold is used.

This example is a conditional-dependence/recovery example, not evidence of a violated global marginal constraint. All states remain globally realizable.

## 6. Adversarial interpretation controls

The diagonal parity family is a classical probability distribution embedded as a density matrix. Its nonzero D means that nonzero output alone cannot be marketed as uniquely quantum, entanglement-specific, or gravitational.

The noncommuting example likewise does not supply a law changing the globally admissible set. A source functional evaluated on a compatible state is not a source-induced change in admissibility. No earlier fixed-admissibility obstruction is bypassed.

A useful further correction to the research language is essential: a physical theory is supposed to imply its own predictions. The problem with `A phi=M s/2` is not algebraic implication itself. It is counting agreement between mutually constructed quantities as independent validation. Separately obtained source data and separately obtained geometric observations could test an algebraically predicted relation. This screen supplies neither such observation.
