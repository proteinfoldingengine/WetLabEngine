Why compare joint weights, not only the final state?

For correlated inputs, an outcome's conditional weight depends on which other records are available. Those conditional factors can differ between schedules. Their product—the joint branch weight—and the retained state at a matching completed-event set agree in the independent-operation model. An equal-length prefix with different records is not a valid comparison point.

Sensitivity control: deliberately break independence

This is an explicit rejected comparison model, not an added physical interaction or a gravitational force. It verifies that the test detects an unjustified reordering rather than reporting agreement automatically.

What is supplied and what remains open?

The four-qubit carrier, finite motion generators, two local dependency chains, retained algebras and actual outcomes are supplied illustrative data. Reversible pre-time motion is preserved. The model does not derive the first-collapse choice, physical entropy production, durations, global simultaneity or gravity. No entropy functional or downstream score selects the source, motion or record.

The algebraic reason for agreement is that cross-stream branch operators commute. This is an integrated demonstration of the earlier conditional partial-order structure, not a newly discovered quantum law.
