Why the full family requires the full algebra

Every starting algebra already contains the diagonal rank-one matrix units in the fixed input basis. One conjugated rank-one projector has all its matrix entries nonzero. Multiplying it on the left and right by those diagonal units extracts every matrix unit. Any containing algebra closed under multiplication must therefore be the full 16×16 algebra. This argument is specific to the supplied motion and basis; it is not a universal claim that reduced physics is impossible.

Minimum algebra is stronger than minimum prediction space

A linear span of I, X and Z can track an orbit of a qubit Z observable. But XZ produces the missing Y direction, so closure under multiplication requires the full qubit algebra. We compute the minimum invariant unital *-algebra, not the smallest arbitrary sufficient statistic or the earlier geometry-aware GOSM module.

Why this is not recovered information

The larger description specifies information that would have needed to remain available BEFORE pruning. Reading an already pruned state with a bigger set of observables does not recreate the discarded distinction. Two equal retained inputs remain equal. Enlargement can also make old central record projections noncentral: their observables remain present, but they no longer define independent classical sectors of the larger algebra. This is a diagnostic requirement, not an adopted record-preserving physical repair or a law choosing what nature retains.
