Why identical classical data do not prove collapse

For the supplied operations, the coherent encoding has record blocks equal to the earlier branch operators. Measuring the record labels therefore yields the same joint probabilities and conditional system states for any input. This is an implementation of known deferred-measurement machinery, not a new physical law.

Keeping joint coherence permits a full unitary inverse. Explicitly pinching record sectors maps a computed pair of orthogonal inputs to indistinguishable outputs, so no single recovery map can recover both from that retained description. This is a scoped information-loss statement, not proof of objective destruction in every possible physical extension.
