# Nested-chain non-stacking repair below outer saturation
Status: proposed general lemma; requires independent review and preregistered validation.

Let a no-unary rooted tree have exactly three internal vertices R,U,V on a chain. R has a-1 side leaves and child U; U has b-1 side leaves and child V; V has c leaf children, with a,b,c>=2. The root palette is K={0,...,k-1}, k>=2. Nonroot supports are nonempty subsets of their parent's, moves change one label incidence while preserving admission, and q is the hitting-number profile. Fix an attained target (r,s,t) at R,U,V.

## General restricted claim
If r<a, every state in the target fiber has a path to a common canonical state with sum_v |q_v-q_v*|<=1. Therefore B1,Binf,Bs are independently0 within each exact component and independently1 between distinct components. LEX is separately(1,1,1) between distinct components. This lemma is not asserted when r=a, and makes no assertion for arbitrary retained trees.

## Outer scaffold
Put l=a-1, S=support(U), and let h be the hitting number of the l outer side leaves. Since adding one child S changes hitting number by at most1, h<=r<=h+1. Freeze every support in the nested subtree while normalizing only the outer side leaves by the reviewed v16.43 star construction on palette K, target h. Its canonical endpoint has singleton colors0,...,h-1 on the first h side leaves and0 on the rest.

If l,k,h are not all equal, this star normalization is exact in h, so outer hitting remains in {h,h+1} and its deviation from r is at most1. If l=k=h, then r=h=k (the whole profile has a cover K), and the reviewed fully packed star transpositions only lower side hitting to h-1. Outer hitting stays between h-1 and h, again with deviation at most1. Degenerate l=1 normalization is exact: add chosen color0 then remove other incidences, never emptying the leaf. None of these moves changes s or t.

Expand S to K one missing label at a time. Descendant supports are unchanged, so s and t stay exact. With side hitting h, outer hitting remains h or h+1. At the end S=K, and outer hitting is exactly h. Because r<a, we have r<=l. If h=r-1, then r>=2 and the side leaf at zero-based side-leaf ordinal r-1 is a redundant0 leaf. Since the attained target r<=k, label r-1 exists. Add color r-1 there and then remove0. The original singleton0 anchor remains. During this step side hitting is h or h+1=r, and S=K makes outer hitting equal the side hitting. If h=r, no operation is needed. Thus the scaffold ends with S=K, canonical r-color outer side leaves, and outer hitting exactly r. Total deviation throughout was at most1, at R only.

## Nested repair behind the scaffold
The subtree rooted at U is exactly an admitted two-internal-vertex tree with root palette K, root degree b, inner degree c, and target (s,t). Apply the reviewed v16.44 general normalization to it. Every local move lifts to one admitted global incidence move: U stays K, no outer side leaf changes, and all subtree supports remain nested. The outer profile depends only on U's support and the outer side leaves; it therefore remains exactly r throughout. v16.44 bounds total deviation inside the subtree by1. Thus deviations cannot stack between R and the nested subtree.

The final canonical state is: U=K; outer side leaves use first r distinct labels then0; inside U use the v16.44 canonical state. Explicitly, if s<=b-1, V uses labels0,...,t-1 and middle side leaves use first s labels then0. If s=b, V uses labels b-1,...,b+t-2 and the b-1 middle side leaves use distinct labels0,...,b-2. Feasibility in this second case follows from the attained disjoint-child profile s=b, which implies k>=b-1+t. The c bottom leaves use the first t distinct labels of V's chosen palette, then its first label. This endpoint depends only on the target and fixed indexing, not the input state.

Reverse one canonical normalization and concatenate with another. The same L1<=1 bound holds. Distinct exact components exclude a zero-cost path for all three objectives; their integer values and the common upper path give equality1. This establishes the claimed general restricted lemma independently of the finite audit.

## Exact remaining obstruction
When r=a=l+1, attaining the number of outer children forces their supports to be pairwise disjoint: a shared label could hit two children and reduce the cover below a. It also forces h=l. Expanding U to K makes outer hitting h=a-1, so the outer defect persists while a nested repair might itself need an excursion. The scaffold above cannot restore outer hitting r with U=K, since l side leaves have hitting number at most l. This shows a limitation of this construction, not that two simultaneous deviations are necessary on every path. The frozen complete threshold audit tests alternatives in this saturated class. Universal unit barriers and general saturated-chain repair remain unresolved unless separately proved or refuted; finite success alone cannot settle them.
