A static-coordinate graph allows only one selected q coordinate to vary by at most1; the complete zero-q fiber is contained in every such graph. Appending zero-cost within-component paths proves representative independence. A failed static-coordinate cut does not prove nonunit behavior. All failed-coordinate cuts together, plus a full L1<=1 path, would prove that the location of the single deviation must change. The present finite test does not prove unrestricted unit universality.
