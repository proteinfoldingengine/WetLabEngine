## Claim discipline

Forensic records distinguish:

1. executed/raw evidence;
2. executable source and exact provenance;
3. contemporaneous narrative claims;
4. later synthesis and interpretation.

A missing source artifact is not treated as a negative experimental result. It is treated as a provenance boundary until the exact generating source is recovered and pinned.
