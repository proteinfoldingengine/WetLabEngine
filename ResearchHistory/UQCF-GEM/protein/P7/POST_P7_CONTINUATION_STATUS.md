## Final bounded decision

`NO_GO_CONTINUE_RECOVERED_HISTORICAL_CUSTOM_PROTEIN_MECHANISMS`

This decision is narrower than “stop protein research.”

It means that, after forensic recovery and prospective falsification, the **recoverable historical custom mechanism families** have not earned further mechanism-specific development.

## Why the P6 open question is now resolved

`PHASE-I OBSERVABLE/DAG CONTROLLER — OPEN PENDING P7`.

The recovered source does not preserve the broad adaptive multi-force mechanism suggested by retrospective summaries:

- the first phase is skipped before any optimizer step;
- the fractal “force” has no coordinate-gradient path;
- the only later adaptive coordinate-force addition is contact springs.

That remaining mechanism class had already been isolated in the later Patch630 line and failed held-out transfer on 1CRN.

Therefore P7 does not justify a new historical-mechanism folding campaign.

## Reopen rule

Do not reopen the historical line because another coefficient, seed, contact cutoff, gate threshold or target appears promising.

A historical-mechanism branch may be reopened only if newly recovered evidence demonstrates a **materially different mechanism** that was not represented in P1/P4/P5/P7 or the held-out contact tests—for example, an exact Patch622/624 raw runner/log showing additional active force switching that contradicts the certified P7 source reduction.

A newly invented observable-driven controller may also be scientifically studied, but it is a **new hypothesis**, not validation of the historical engine.

## Scope boundary

This closeout does not claim:

- that torsional information is never useful;
- that adaptive controllers cannot help folding;
- that custom protein physics is impossible;
- that the preserved infrastructure lacks scientific value.

It says the **specific historical mechanisms we could recover and test** do not presently justify further rescue/development.
