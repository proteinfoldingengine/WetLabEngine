# Protein / WetLabEngine Scientific Program — Final Closeout

**Closeout date:** 2026-09-18  
**Final decision:** `CLOSE_CUSTOM_PROTEIN_MECHANISM_PROGRAM`

## Decision

The novelty-focused protein mechanism program is closed on the current evidence.

This is a research-program decision, not a theorem that no useful protein model can ever be built from the retained codebase.

The validated infrastructure, provenance archive, historical source, constrained peptide kinematics, statistical controls, and bounded partial signals remain worth preserving.

## Why the program is now closed

The final audit deliberately separated established prior art from potentially distinctive claims.

Broad candidate ideas in the archive — folding funnels, compaction, contact/topological order, local torsional preorganization, adaptive schedules, and generic path dependence/non-Markovianity — are not by themselves sufficiently novel to justify further custom-mechanism development.

The last archive-supported candidate with a plausible distinct scientific claim was:

> retained multiscale bridge/coherence history carries transferable future-realizability information beyond the current reduced state, elapsed time, and ordinary generic history.

That candidate was frozen as P9A and prospectively adjudicated.

P9A returned:

`NO_GO_RETAINED_COHERENCE_TRANSFER_SIGNAL`

The retained-coherence features:

- improved held-out RMSE by only 2.6% in 1UAO -> 1L2Y, with permutation p ~ 0.464;
- worsened held-out RMSE by 5.6% in 1L2Y -> 1UAO, with permutation p ~ 0.488.

The result does not justify P9B or further retained-coherence development.

## Earlier NO-GOs remain scoped, not erased

This closeout does not broaden any individual experiment beyond what it actually tested.

- P1 remains a NO-GO for hierarchical-v9-specific advantage.
- P4 remains a NO-GO for the physical interpretation of the invalid historical Patch-630 Cartesian backbone.
- P5 remains a NO-GO for incremental value of the recovered TPO entropy term.
- P6 remains a NO-GO for the strong transferable sequence-order-specific preorganization claim, while preserving its bounded real-sequence versus generic-collapse signal.
- P7's exact source reduction remains valid for the source it audited; P8 correctly reopened the broader historical-source classification.
- P8B remains a NO-GO for its frozen representation transfer. The later calibration criticism means it should not be promoted into a theorem against every physically faithful realization.

The final program closeout does not depend on pretending P8B answered more than it did. It follows because, after the novelty/prior-art filter, no surviving archive-supported mechanism has earned another scientifically valuable prospective gate.

## What is preserved

Preserve:

- all raw authoritative artifacts and hashes;
- source manifests and historical Drive provenance;
- constrained canonical peptide-backbone implementation;
- corrected structural evaluators;
- native-information firewalls;
- matched-control and deterministic-seed infrastructure;
- exact sign-flip / multiplicity tooling;
- CI RED/GREEN certification patterns;
- P6 bounded partial signal;
- P8 historical source-forensics correction;
- documentation of the P8B representation-transfer caveat;
- P9 final falsifier and result.

These may be useful as research infrastructure or historical evidence without implying a surviving custom folding mechanism.

## Reopening rule

Do not reopen for:

- coefficient tuning;
- alternate history windows;
- more v9/TPO/Phase-I combinations;
- recalibration whose primary purpose is to reverse P8B;
- a new endpoint on the same trajectories;
- additional conventional force-field terms;
- another historical replay.

Reopen only if **materially new independent evidence** appears, such as:

1. a newly recovered immutable historical artifact that establishes a mechanism materially distinct from the audited families and supplies a new falsifiable prediction;
2. external experimental data that specifically distinguishes a proposed retained/coherence mechanism from established reaction-coordinate or non-Markovian explanations;
3. an independently derived mechanism with a preregistered prediction that was not selected from these results;
4. a new physical measurement showing a transferable effect that cannot be reduced to ordinary geometry, topology, energy-landscape, or history-augmented models.
