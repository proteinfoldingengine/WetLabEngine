## Disclosed deduction before proof freeze

For one even directed role cycle of length m>=8, let H_alt be the m/2 cyclic four-windows whose start positions have one parity. For every other four-set J, declare an original mask Q_J=G minus J.

Then four-covers appear to be exactly H_alt. Thus exact target four holds, but the one-step rotation sends every H_alt window to the opposite parity, so

    H_alt intersect pi^{-1}(H_alt)=empty.

The cycle has exactly alternating good/bad starts. Its duplicate-insensitive bad-window count is m/2, exactly the sharp boundary at which adjacency need not occur. Each bad window is missed by exactly its own complementary root.

Pair redundancy appears to be

    rho=binomial(m-2,2)-2,

already far above X40's lower threshold. Replacing every original root by d labelled copies leaves the cover family and obstruction unchanged while multiplying rho by d. Therefore neither exact-four consistency nor arbitrarily large raw shared-witness redundancy can by itself force an X46-admissible cycle or X45 representative compatibility.

The proposed positive clarification is to distinguish

    beta(C)=|union_i B_i(C)|

from X46's Gamma(C)=sum_i|B_i(C)|. Beta is invariant under duplicate roots and is the exact number of bad cyclic windows. Beta<ceil(m/2) forces adjacent good windows and is sharp as a count-only condition; equality admits the alternating obstruction. The exact adjacency test remains stronger than either scalar.
