## What is being repaired

Three logically different tasks must remain distinct:
1. A legal local edit must preserve floors and a witness against each forbidden small hitting set.
2. Renewable protection must survive handover and furnish the NEXT eligible edit.
3. Complete repair must have finite progress and restore the exact labelled destination.

A safe first edit or a supplied finite safe sequence is not universal next-move existence.

## Consolidated exact completion chain

For uniform original floor h and feasible exact-q endpoints q>=3:

Choose a minimum endpoint q-cover and compact each support to h labels retaining a cover incidence. Every compaction primitive is exact q. Select actual guards, using the smaller incidence-derived or candidate strict classical bound.

If the chosen guard sizes satisfy r>=m_A+m_C-1, the destination guard fits on existing slots with at most one source overlap. Uniform floors permit a permutation of the FULL exact destination. Accepted native root-swap Lemma M realizes that permutation safely; its reverse restores labels to their original slots later.

During preparation the old guard is fixed. During the shared edit every forbidden small hitting set of the exclusive supports must miss both shared supports, hence their union and every current subset. Afterward the actual new guard is fixed and protects every remaining root repair.

Every unfinished phase provides an eligible addition or deletion; total symmetric difference to the permuted destination strictly decreases. This reaches the exact permuted destination through a finite lower-guard path. It can exceed q: apply accepted maximum-layer Theorem A to that supplied path. Then reverse the endpoint permutation and reverse destination compaction. Each join is exact q, so the final path remains in {q-1,q} and restores the original labelled noncompact destination.

Protection can be renewed at q-1. What returns at handover is sufficient protection for continued progress, not necessarily exact q. Exactness is nevertheless essential at both original entries: it supplies the minimum cover, omitted-label guard, candidate replacement root and safe full-endpoint permutation.

## Why the X9 candidate is a general improvement

The classical guard bound B=binomial(h+q-2,h) concerns arbitrary critical (q-1)-families. An exact-q endpoint contains MORE protection than a standalone critical guard.

A minimum-cardinality guard attaining B is forced by classical equality to contain every h-subset of a core of h+q-2 labels. A (q-1)-cover of that core cannot cover the exact endpoint, so another actual root misses it. Select two core h-supports containing that root's core intersection; dropping those two from the selection and selecting the existing external root still excludes every size-(q-2) cover. The new actual guard is smaller.

That removes assumed availability at the old extremal guard count. It does not edit a bare guard or assume a small buffer can universally renew. The resulting candidate bound B-1 composes with incidence availability and O1, yielding r>=2B-3 independently of palette size. Seven-label arithmetic is a consequence, not the research architecture.
