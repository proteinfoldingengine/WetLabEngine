## Why the second actor follows, without a designated pair
For old/new admitted degree-four graphs, the minimum is the same: each has an edge wholly outside the two edited rows, and stationary weights are identical. At every stationary chart the number of edges to the edited pair is unchanged. An old such edge necessarily moves by a nonzero squared grade in a singly changed row, so it must cease to be minimal and be replaced by the edge to the other actor. In particular the two actors have no old common stationary neighbor.

If the actors bordered one another in the old graph, each must trade the three external neighbors, and the mutual edge remains. In the old neighborhood of the first actor, the other actor then has equal work to each of those three neighbors: all three are lowered to the same minimum by the other actor's single edit of one fixed squared grade. One of those three pairs must be one of the two old disjoint opposite maxima, hence all three would be maximal, contradiction. Thus the two actors are nonadjacent and each must receive the other's four neighbors. The new mutual edge cannot be minimal since all four slots are already accounted for.

For the first ordinary withdrawal, work at the source row changes by +4 when the stationary chart holds S and −4 otherwise. Its exactly four minima become the four old neighbors of b by construction. Consequently the only possible second actor is the one whose old four neighbors form that set (distinct neighborhoods on this torus). The repair weight is 4, since B's old work to the four old neighbors of the source was uniformly mu+4 and must fall to the minimum (the positive grade is therefore 2). Other grade leaves cannot repair. Any undo at the source is outside the distinct actor case.

For each of the two first withdrawals compare the same entire initial view family, same source a, and distinct orbit leaves: S and its x-reflection. The reflected recipient is c=(-3,3), different from b. At each target an exact footprint scan over the 484 distinct original grade-2 masks plus the one modified source mask finds four second edits passing the changed-row minimum test, all additions. Of these, the original v15.43 builder rejects three and accepts only the addition of the *modified source mask*, using the actual same leaf; it is unique because only that leaf has shrunk to 24 holders. The successful destinations have 121 cycles and exact admitted flat baselines. The separate coordinate checker reproduces those four cases and the same-leaf result without importing the producer. At center 1 each of the three wrong alternatives gives a sole maximum (2, recipient), excess 20, not two disjoint maxima. At chart b, the common prior full P1 record is literally the same for the two arms. In the first it gains the exact grade-2 root leaf, reducing Pin grade by 2 under inherited finite-additive P1; in the second it does not change at all. No claim that a physical receiver can read either state. Source-only controls fail degree four; unearned restore/occurrence must not be inferred from legality of the two successful endpoints.
