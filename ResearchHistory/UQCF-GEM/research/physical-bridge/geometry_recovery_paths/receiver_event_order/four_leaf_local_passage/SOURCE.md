# Identifying the changed incidence without naming a source actor

In the six-chart preparation of RESULT.md, assume either no initial withdrawal or exactly one legal chart-leaf withdrawal, followed by the original flat-star normalization. Begin B's local record immediately at the common preparation. A first loss that leaves exactly two leaves identifies **A's withdrawal of leaf 1** among all ten possible withdrawals, including the two that could have been made by B itself. No chart name, chosen source pair, local phase marker or externally synchronized step needs to be supplied to B's readout. The inference presumes the known preparation, source menu and invoked operation.

The complete set of initial minimum covers is AC and AB. Only A holds three leaves. Without A, covering leaf 1 uses a chart holding that leaf alone, so two charts cannot cover all four leaves. With A, either C or B must supply leaf 4. To destroy every old minimum cover by a single deletion, the edited chart must belong to both. The deleted leaf must also be present in neither partner, C nor B. Only leaf 1 qualifies. Its two other holders keep the union intact, and its withdrawal raises q from 2 to 3. All ten incidences are legal because every initial leaf has at least two holders. The same identification survives arbitrary renaming: the source is the only three-leaf chart, and the site is the only leaf absent from both of its minimum-cover partners. The historical v16.26 trigger is true on just this one edit.

If the unique critical event occurs, B is remote and its first deletion during the existing normalization leaves two leaves. If one of the seven other legal non-B withdrawals occurs, or the caller runs on the prior itself, q=2 and B's first deletion leaves three. If B itself makes the initial withdrawal, B began with two leaves and sees its own first deletion leave one before normalization. Thus B can use its very first loss from the preparation, even without a boundary marker between an initial cut and the program. The table classifies observations, not the probability of each event.

source.py calls the original atomic withdrawal for every incidence and replays each arm and the unchanged control under each of 24 leaf orders and 12 cyclic/reversed chart orders. The full-order result follows from the branch proof; the separately enumerated 17,280-order test concerns the two main arms. The source ledger contains all 11 cases. At Pin grade one and leaf grade two the three readout counts correspond to local Pin values 5, 3 and 7 respectively.

The one-withdrawal scope matters. Withdrawing leaves 2 then 3 at A can yield q=3 without removing leaf 1. B's first deletion again leaves two in the direct control. Neither A's unique possible strict increment nor B's eventual readout selects the event for enactment. The v16.26 producer loops through candidate withdrawals and counts their triggers. Calling the stock R routine on the original q=2 state begins with an addition during root expansion. These callers do not supply a rule that spontaneously performs the unique initial withdrawal.
