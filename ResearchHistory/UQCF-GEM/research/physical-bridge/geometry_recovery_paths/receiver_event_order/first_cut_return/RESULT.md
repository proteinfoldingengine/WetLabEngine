# Which holder of a leaf can initiate a one-edit handoff?

On the prepared 121-chart square torus, a grade-2 leaf has 25 original holders. Exactly **one** can withdraw it and have a different chart restore the admitted geometry in one further primitive edit. That holder is recognizable before the cut: it is the unique holder whose entire radius-two neighborhood in the original minimum-work graph also holds the leaf. Five holders contain their radius-one ball. Four of those five fail the radius-two test and cannot hand off the leaf in a single repair.

For every one of the 25 possible initiating holders, the sole same-chart one-edit return is the exact undo of that withdrawal. For the unique eligible holder there is a second possibility: one other chart acquires that exact leaf. All other gains and all withdrawals fail to restore full admission in one edit. This removes the earlier assumption that the second actor differs from the first, while retaining the one-edit full-return premise. Two arbitrary legal edits need not complete a return.

## Domain and counting

We reuse the [reviewed symmetric fixture](../symmetric_matched_event/RESULT.md), including its deliberately designed relative supports, target work profile, and positive grades. The common Pin lies in every view. There are 968 indexed grade-2 leaves, occupying 484 support masks twice each. The other grades in the preparation differ from 2. Each grade-2 leaf has 25 holders. A single primitive adds or removes one chart-leaf incidence; each withdrawal considered here preserves the union.

Translation lets us fix the first chart at 0 and enumerate every grade-2 leaf it can legally withdraw: **200 indexed choices, 100 distinct masks**. Only eight choices (four distinct masks) admit any one-edit remote return. Their four recipients have displacements (±3,±3). Under the archive's indexing these are charts 36, 41, 91 and 96. Each eligible mask has only one recipient. Counting translations gives 968 eligible out of 24,200 indexed first withdrawals on the full prepared orbit. This is a combinatorial fraction; the model supplies no sampling law. No particular absolute chart or absolute leaf is selected.

## Exhaustiveness

Write μ for the original least pair work. An admitted degree-four graph before and after two incidence edits has an edge wholly outside the edited charts at both ends of the comparison. Each such pair has unchanged work, so the two minima must agree. If both edits are at chart 0, every stationary vertex keeps all its stationary neighbors. Its remaining possible edge to chart 0 must then have precisely its old membership. Hence chart 0 must recover its entire old neighbor set. Each of its four old minimum edges moved by ±4 in the first cut. The magnitude of a second incidence change is the square of the edited grade, so only grade 2 can restore those edges. At each after-cut state we test all 968 indexed leaves via their current masks with exact multiplicities and the legal sign prescribed by current membership. For each initial mask the **only** degree-preserving same-chart candidate is the gain of its one modified leaf. That candidate restores the entire initial atlas.

For two different edited charts the inherited degree and opposite-pair argument forces the actors to be nonadjacent, have disjoint old neighborhoods and trade those neighborhoods. Before scanning leaves we test every possible other actor against the entire first-cut source row outside the mutual entry. That entry can change again, so it is excluded from this first screen. Only the four eligible masks have a surviving actor. In each case that actor previously had work μ+4 to every old neighbor of chart 0. Therefore its required second grade is also 2. Testing the complete changed row leaves four candidate support masks per eligible arm. The pinned full carrier rejects three for ambiguous opposite pairing and admits only the gain of the modified leaf, which has multiplicity one. Restoring this leaf at another chart changes the indexed atlas even though the regular square geometry returns.

An independent coordinate implementation reconstructs the complete domain and every degree-surviving candidate using cut differences. It also checks opposite maxima, chart labels, multiplicities, and all 25 possible initiators of one fixed leaf by translation. As an additional characterization identified during exploration, take the subset of holders containing all four immediate neighbors. Exactly one of those five is surrounded by four other such holders. This is precisely the full radius-two-ball test. The square-group action carries both the criterion and the successful role with the leaf throughout the orbit. The equivalence is proved for this constructed family; other support shapes can behave differently.

## What runs before any repair

The two originally reported arms have different distance-two neighborhoods. We traced the v15.40 comparator on their actual work tables against the unchanged regular incidence target. On the initial table it calls its distance routine. At either first cut it finds that the minimum-edge set differs and short-circuits before computing graph distances. Its metric test still passes. In a direct diagnostic call to the v15.43 projection evaluator with the irregular payload, the operational-complex constructor returns the degree failure and no field evaluation runs. These are traces of the supplied component calls; no archived process taking a spontaneous first cut is claimed. The separately computed radius-two test above uses the **initial regular graph and a chosen leaf support**, not an unobserved distance calculation by the historical irregular-cut caller.

The model permits a stop after withdrawal and permits a legal nonrestoring move, for instance a second withdrawal from the initiating chart of the still-present duplicate leaf with the original support. The guard classifies candidate endpoints. Neither that guard nor the conditional uniqueness theorem starts a transfer. The open operational question is whether an existing process uses the leaf's support to initiate the cut or reaches and enacts the non-undo return. Physical access remains open.
