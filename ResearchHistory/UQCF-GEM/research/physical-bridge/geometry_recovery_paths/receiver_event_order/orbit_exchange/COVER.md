# The original atomic cover receipt remains constant throughout

In this one-level Pin tree, a set of charts covers the root children exactly when it meets every leaf's holder set. Historical v16.26 tau is the minimum size of such a cover, h is the same value on this star, and a withdrawal's trigger is true precisely when all old minimum witnesses fail after that cut.

Freeze the 967 other leaves. Every distinct original support still occurs, including the duplicate of the changing leaf. The resulting set of **cover constraints** is invariant under all translations. Individual indexed multiplicities need not remain invariant. Let K be a minimum cover of these fixed constraints, of size k. For every nonempty current support T, choose a point in K and translate it to any point of T. The translated K still hits all fixed constraints and also hits T. Adding the T constraint therefore leaves the optimum at k. This proves exact constancy for every nonempty T, including every intermediate state in both 121-edit orders. On any legal withdrawal T to T' contained in T, a k-cover hitting T' was also an old k-cover hitting T. Therefore the original loss-of-all-witnesses flag is false and delta_h is zero on every such withdrawal. Each primitive addition is the reverse of a withdrawal with the same zero increment. No numeric value for k on 121 charts is needed.

The entire endpoint families of covers, including nonminimum covers, also agree. The fixed constraints contain S and at least one support disjoint from S (the full representative orbit has 15 distinct disjoint masks). Any fixed cover must therefore meet both S and its complement. Each endpoint adds a redundant constraint. This last assertion is for the endpoints, whereas constancy of the numerical optimum and survival of some old witness at each deletion hold throughout. The identities of the surviving witnesses may vary.

The two collective paths thus remain exact with respect to the original local cover count while their interior nearest-work graphs fail admission. The archived three-chart-style normalizer is not being run on this 968-child root. The cover data supply no positive trigger to advance either path. The algebra above specializes the original receipt definitions to this fixture; it does not claim that we enumerated every large minimum witness.

Direct tests of the original h, tau_at and atomic on nine smaller transitive interval orbits (three to five charts) cover 183 mutable supports and 402 legal withdrawals. In all 402 the original increment is zero and the trigger false. With a nontransitive fixed singleton at chart 0 and one additional leaf initially shared by charts 0 and 1, withdrawal by chart 0 changes the cover from one to two and the original trigger becomes true.
