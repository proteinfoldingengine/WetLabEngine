Remove every compensating pair and singleton leaf from the earlier [symmetric preparation](../symmetric_matched_event/RESULT.md). Keep the common Pin and its 968 indexed grade-2 leaves, the 11-by-11 chart group, and the supplied 25-chart footprint with all its translates, rotations and reflections. The stripped family generates a regular square carrier from the inherited work. A transfer between just two charts in two edits cannot return it to admission. Yet if only one indexed leaf may change and a different admitted state is eventually reached, **every chart must flip its membership in that leaf**. The complete pair-work matrix then agrees exactly with the starting matrix. The two states are distinguishable in each chart's own retained grades even if leaf names are omitted.

## Proof of the two endpoints for one variable leaf

Write S for the original holder set and T for any current nonempty holder set of that leaf. All other leaves and the common Pin are fixed. A pair changes its work by four times the difference between its crossing indicators for T and S. This is always −4, 0, or +4, even after arbitrarily many edits. Thus every originally non-neighbor still has greater work than every original nearest pair. The new minimum graph is a subgraph of the original four-regular graph. If the new graph is to have degree four everywhere it must keep every original edge. All these edges must consequently change work by the same amount.

Some original nearest edges cross S, and others have both endpoints on the same side. The former cannot increase their crossing indicator, and the latter cannot decrease it. Therefore the common change must be zero. On each original edge (i,j), the XOR of the two endpoint memberships is preserved. Equivalently the indicator of membership having flipped is equal at i and j. Connectedness makes this indicator constant on all 121 charts. The only possibilities are T=S and T equal to the complement of S. Conversely both choices preserve every pair's work and pass the entire original carrier builder. The proof uses neither a chosen final holder set nor a group action on chart labels. It is a consequence of a connected regular minimum graph, the separating work gap, and the two kinds of original minimum edge. It applies within the one-variable-leaf premise.

The distinct return thus needs at least 121 primitive edits. Both archived 121-edit orders begin with the specified withdrawal at chart 0, keep the union covered at every step, and flip each chart exactly once. The first adds the 96 outsiders before deleting the remaining original copies. The second temporarily keeps just one old holder, adds a new holder, and then releases that old copy. An addition is an inverse-certified incidence type and a removal preserves the union, since these are root leaves and each intermediate support stays nonempty. The ledger checks all 120 interior states per path by counting minimum edges. None is admitted. The pinned builder rejects the first cut with degree_not_four. These are paths of union-preserving primitives through irregular geometries. They are not paths required to preserve the historical normalizer's excursion budget or a prediction that a procedure will traverse 121 edits.

At the distinct return, 25 old holders lost the leaf and 96 peers gained it. Each old holder's leaf count goes from 200 to 199 and its P1 Pin grade from 1537 to 1539. For each former nonholder these go to 201 and 1535. The original P1 is checked directly at representatives 0 and 36. The shared starting count and extensive pushforward give the other charts by the same arithmetic. The fine grades remain fixed. This is a locally distinguishable change of holding a grade-2 leaf, not a new unknown numerical grade supplied by the source. The changed holder multiplicity (25 to 96) also rules out a mere relabeling of this prepared incidence family.

## What shorter paths can and cannot achieve with other leaves available

For equal-grade leaves, work in units of four has the parity of the sum of the two chart leaf counts. In any admitted state on 121 charts its minimum cannot be odd: the nearest graph would be bipartite by parity, and a positive regular bipartite graph has equally many vertices in its two parts. At an even minimum, connectedness makes every chart's count parity identical. Starting with identical parities, fewer than 121 single flips cannot reverse every chart's parity. Each chart must therefore undergo an even number of edits at any such return. As a control, the 16 words of length four with Hamming nearest neighbors give an even-order connected four-regular bipartite graph with both count parities. This control concerns the parity argument only.

For a handoff of the representative leaf from chart 0 to an initially absent chart, completing in four edits requires two toggles at each actor. The other toggle at each actor can concern any one of the 967 other indexed leaves. The 96 possible receiving charts all lie outside the original four nearest neighbors of 0. Even after four arbitrary toggles the work gap rules out any new nearest pair. An unaffected minimum edge fixes the minimum at an admitted endpoint. Chart 0's source loss raises work to **each** old neighbor by four. To cancel all four rises, its single counterflip must switch away from its initial bit on a leaf whose membership differs from chart 0 at every neighbor. No such leaf exists. This excludes all 89,768,544 four-edit endpoint choices by a common row constraint. It includes all 15,936 indexed reciprocal exchanges, including both earlier nominated receivers.

The wider local constraint is visible directly in the counts. Of the 968 leaf incidences at any chart, the number of occupied neighbors among its four neighbors has distribution:

| Chart holds leaf | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |

| No | 464 | 256 | 48 | 0 | 0 |

Thus each initial bit agrees with a weak majority of the four neighbors. With those four rows held at their initial values at the endpoint, changing any bit at chart 0 can only increase or preserve the sum of its four old edge works. Losing this representative leaf strictly increases the sum, because all four neighbors hold it. No number of other changes confined in their **net effect** to chart 0 and one initially absent receiver can both leave the leaf transferred and recover the original labeled adjacency. A stationary old edge bounds the final minimum from above. Temporary edits elsewhere that fully undo do not change this endpoint argument. This is a constraint on the prepared geometry; no majority-vote writer is present here.

With freely editable leaves the unrestricted minimum length for this handoff to any initially absent chart lies between **6 and 121**. Six is a lower bound, not an attained minimum. Independently, swapping the full rows of chart 0 and chart 13 gives an admitted, genuinely rewired indexed graph; adding required copies before releases (retaining at least one holder per leaf) realizes the 576 differing incidence toggles. The designated initial withdrawal can also be taken first since 24 other copies protect that leaf. No global optimality of this rewiring route is claimed.
