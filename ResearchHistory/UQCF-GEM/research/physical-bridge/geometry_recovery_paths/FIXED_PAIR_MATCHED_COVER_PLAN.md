# Fixed-pair control with equal cover measurements

The primary fixed-pair run at preregistration `7b83a729f7d40f53fbad1a002010ff65a3e44ce4` has already produced 864 joint-root contrasts in 1,944 matched comparisons. In the prior-unique six-leaf example the two arms have different post-event cover orders, 3 and 2. The primary raw file is already computed (uncompressed SHA-256 `7287e1013c05b3b6a32642f936184b923d4cd16ed1d4e9d2964852feb536e7b9`). This supplement is prospective for its additional comparisons on that existing census.

Regroup all 1,944 equal-grade pairs by whether their complete ordered numerical payloads agree chart by chart, whether their v16.26 cover transitions agree, whether the joint resolved root differs, and whether R is the unique partner of the specified source among all minimum two-chart covers containing S before the event. Preserve the identities of all pairs in every class. For a star the local root cover order is the global order. Native `tau_before`, `tau_after`, `h_before`, `h_after`, `delta_h` and `trigger` must all agree when calling two source events cover-matched.

The four-leaf support masks (6,3,1,6), in chart bit order R,S,H, supply an analytical candidate. Leaves 1 and 2 carry grade 1. Leaf 3 is private to R, and H can replace S in a global cover. The expected joint roots are 4 versus 3 at fine root grade 3, with cover order 2 and no trigger on either arm. R is selectable before either cut from the geometry and the identity of S. Check the candidate with the actual operators and an independent calculation. A different analytical consequence is that a globally unique minimum prior pair on a star cannot exhibit a mixed shared/exclusive contrast with the same post-cut order. Test this too. No exact census counts for the new classes have been computed at the time of this supplement.
