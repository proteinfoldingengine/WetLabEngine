## 2. What has been established

For the prepared X2 carrier with two singleton anchors, two complementary guards, their valid original floors, a fixed finite palette and untouched residual roots:

1. Exact cover transfer: T0=T1=T2, T3=T0 union T6, and T4=T5=T6. Hence the hitting-number sequence is (a,a,a,min(a,b),b,b,b), where a and b are the two prepared endpoint values.
2. Direct protected-band criterion: the prescribed handover stays in 3<=tau<=4 if and only if both endpoints are in that band. No maximum-layer-removal call is required for such a handover.
3. Retained input: the derived residual table F_R, with one Boolean entry per unordered label pair, plus declared carrier/current-target/phase information decides the prescribed move legality and updates its own record without consulting individual residual supports again.
4. Repetition: complete safe handovers reach exactly the prepared targets in the same connected component of the ordered-pair graph derived from F_R. A decreasing integer rank supplies termination.
5. A useful sufficient class: if at most three labels hit every residual root, that graph is connected. Any two protected prepared endpoints have a route of at most six handovers, or 36 incidence edits.
6. A precise limit: a sixteen-root residual construction with only two disjoint four-label covers disconnects this macro graph, although both selected endpoints have exact tau=4. A different native path still connects them. This refutes universal scheduling by the prescribed macro, not X2 or general repair.
7. Genuine information loss: two different residual incidence families on the same labelled carrier yield identical retained records and can be handled by the same policy. The policy has not been given the entire incidence state under another name.

The full proofs are Sections 2-7 of the immutable result, not just the assertions in this list.

## 4. Exact edge/refutation record

- Empty residual family: its transversal number is 0; pad its empty cover to a three-set in the existing palette. The positive proof still works. No empty SUPPORT is admitted.
- Minimum palette k=4 and guard floors k-2=2: guards remain of size 2 or 3 at every phase, so no spare-capacity assumption is hidden.
- Source already equals target: the graph path is empty, d=0 and no primitive is needed.
- Swapped anchor targets: the h3 detour in Section 5 handles the case (h2,h1) without violating distinct-anchor syntax.
- Cover contains both old and new anchor labels: it may belong to both endpoint families; Boolean union, not exclusive union, is essential.
- Source-safe but destination-unsafe macro: the cross-block example gives exactly (4,4,4,4,5,5,5). This rejects a claim that the six-move primitive sequence is always band-safe just because X2 is a connectivity theorem.
- Residual tau=4 does not automatically imply policy failure: four singleton residual roots on a four-label palette give an all-true F table. The residual-three condition is sufficient, not necessary.
- Non-injective information reduction does not prove a minimal or always smaller encoding, and initialization of F_R still may need global residual information.
