## Branches

1. **Covariant pruning (D, already covered).** E_B is U(A)-covariant iff uBu* = B for all u ∈ U(A). Under Ad U(A), A = Z(A) ⊕ ⊕_{n_i ≥ 2} sl_{n_i}. The sl_{n_i} summands are irreducible and pairwise inequivalent, and Z(A) carries the trivial action, so every invariant unital *-subalgebra has the form B = W ⊕ ⊕_{i∈S} M_{n_i} with W ⊆ Z(A). (sl_n generates M_n for n ≥ 2, so 1_i ∈ B for i ∈ S.) Any x ∈ Z(B) commutes with each M_{n_j}, j ∈ S, so its block components there are scalar. Hence **Z(B) ⊆ Z(A)**, and by the tower property E_{Z(B)} = E_{Z(B)} ∘ E_{Z(A)}, so E_{Z(B)}(ρ_0 − ρ_1) = 0. This is elementary representation theory, and Theorem A of relational_reference_admission already lists covariant tracial conditional expectations among the blind mechanisms. It is also consistent with covariant_refinement_boundary (covariant laws cannot grow the center). It is recorded as a consistency check, not as a new obstruction.
2. **Noncovariant pruning (C).** Center growth requires B with Z(B) ⊄ Z(A), for example a masa in a block. Then B is the noncentral reference, and the optimum is Theorem B with σ replaced by the selected B-structure. In every inspected source, B is supplied (RAS). In v11.1/v15.11 it is a declared primitive (the I, Z qubit algebra; diag(T, T)). No inherited law selects it, so this is a supplied-reference illustration.
3. **State-dependent selection (D/C).** If B = {ρ_b}'' (the spectral algebra of the actual state), then E_B(ρ_b) = ρ_b: there is no loss and nothing new reaches Z(A)-level access, and the selection map is nonlinear.
