## Standing adversarial comparison and type check

C3 incidence histories and P1 joint isometries are different typed objects. Neither W_R nor W_E literally has a C3 command ledger in the inherited definitions. Therefore it would be invalid to assert that either satisfies the entire C3 ledger contract without supplying an interpretation.

For the portion proposed as a P1 constraint that depends only on the reduced source channel, W_R and W_E agree by the accepted receiver-location result. Such a constraint cannot distinguish them. Event occurrence and signed mathematical notation alone do not add an output-factor constraint.

For the stronger proposed constraint, “R receives a source-discriminating authenticated record,” a blind R is excluded by the meaning of the added receiver-record requirement. But the inspected provenance theorem assumes reception (E3); it does not produce it. This is SUPPLIED, not an inherited elimination of W_E. Moreover, W_R only guarantees the benchmark pair distinction; it must not be credited with full-domain owner representation or a complete C3 ledger.

Exclusive writing addresses unreported source changes. It does not, by itself, identify the destination of a record. Exactly-once correspondence is useful only after the event-to-record association and receiving interface are justified. No new all-realization necessity or minimal physical postulate follows from this audit.
