## Counterexample to T-243 RD-8(iii)

The claim says that for a fixed arbitrary dilation in the declared atom-compatible class, the complete complement K represents y_p on the full simplex iff kappa_pv=kappa_po=0. Its sufficiency paragraph constructs one favorable example; it does not prove sufficiency for every dilation satisfying those two conditions.

Let K=C^2 with orthonormal basis |0>,|1>, and define W on the four orthonormal input atoms:

    W|p> = |p>|0>,    W|u> = |p>|1>,
    W|v> = |v>|1>,    W|o> = |o>|1>.

All four output vectors are orthonormal: the only coincident source-output label is p, and its two complement vectors are orthogonal. Thus W is an isometry. Tr_K(W rho W*) defines a channel C' in precisely RD-8's enlarged class:

    C'(pi_o)=pi_o, C'(pi_p)=pi_p,
    C'(pi_u)=pi_p, C'(pi_v)=pi_v.

The stipulated conditions hold:

    kappa_pv = <1|0> = 0,
    kappa_po = <1|0> = 0.

Nevertheless N_K(pi_u)=N_K(pi_v)=|1><1|, while y_p(pi_u)=1 and y_p(pi_v)=0. No single K effect, and no source-independent decoder on K, can produce these two different values from the same input state. This directly falsifies sufficiency in RD-8(iii). It does not challenge RD-8(i)-(ii), its displayed favorable example, or the accepted fixed-C owner-scope exclusion. This C' differs from that fixed C, exactly as permitted by RD-8's changed quantifier.

## Exact repair proposed for inspection

For this same pure-output dilation class, the complete-complement condition requires the whole owner block, not merely p, to be separated:

    span{e_p,e_u} is orthogonal to span{e_o,e_v}.

Equivalently all four cross-block inner products vanish: <e_o|e_p>, <e_v|e_p>, <e_o|e_u>, <e_v|e_u>.

Necessity follows directly from 0<=M<=I: expectation 1 on a unit vector implies M e=e (since I-M is positive); expectation 0 implies M e=0. Therefore an effect representing the owner indicator on all four pure calibrations has orthogonal 1 and 0 eigenspaces containing the two blocks. Conversely, when the spans are orthogonal, the orthogonal projection onto span{e_p,e_u} gives the required four values and hence every diagonal mixture value by linearity. This repair is an author-side derivation accompanying the challenge, not a fresh independent acceptance.

For a designated proper factor R, the required condition is stronger than complete-complement orthogonality: the support span of tau_p^R and tau_u^R must be orthogonal to the support span of tau_o^R and tau_v^R. The same positive-effect argument applies. Global orthogonal states may have identical R marginals, so the complete-complement condition cannot be promoted into designated receiver ownership.

The favorable RD-8 example can still place K entirely in E and leave an initially blank R unchanged, or place it in R. Thus even the repaired complete-complement condition does not eliminate the standing location freedom. No inherited rule choosing the informative allocation has been identified here.
