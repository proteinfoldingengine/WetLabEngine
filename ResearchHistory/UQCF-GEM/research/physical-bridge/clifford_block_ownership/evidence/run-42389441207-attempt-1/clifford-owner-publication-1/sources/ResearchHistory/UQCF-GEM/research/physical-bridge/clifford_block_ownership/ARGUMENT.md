## 1. Positive result: the local rotation algebras are genuine commuting factors

For each block v with ordered Clifford indices (a,b,c), define
L_v1=-i gamma_b gamma_c, L_v2=-i gamma_c gamma_a, L_v3=-i gamma_a gamma_b,
and R_v=Alg*(I,L_v1,L_v2,L_v3).

Clifford multiplication gives Hermitian L_vk, L_vk^2=I, and
L_vi L_vj=delta_ij I+i epsilon_ijk L_vk.
These four matrices are linearly independent (their normalized trace inner products are delta_ij).
Thus R_v is a unital copy of M_2(C), generated by the already specified local so(3) action.

For distinct blocks the generators commute: moving two Clifford factors through two disjoint factors gives four sign reversals.
Therefore R_A,R_B,R_C commute. Multiplication defines a unital *-homomorphism

Its domain M_8 is simple, so the unital homomorphism is injective.
Consequently

with R_A,R_B,R_C acting on their corresponding factors and Q=(R_A vee R_B vee R_C)' isomorphic to M_2.

## 2. The Clifford-vector effect uses the shared factor

Define Omega_v=-i gamma_a gamma_b gamma_c for each block.
It is Hermitian, Omega_v^2=I, and commutes with every R_w, including w=v.
For distinct v,w, Omega_v Omega_w=-Omega_w Omega_v (nine sign reversals).
Thus each Omega_v lies in Q, is non-scalar, and has zero trace. Two of them generate Q=M_2.

Direct multiplication gives
gamma_a=Omega_v L_v1, gamma_b=Omega_v L_v2, gamma_c=Omega_v L_v3.
Hence for a real unit vector u supported in block v,
Gamma_v(u)=sum_k u_k gamma_vk=Omega_v L_v(u),
E_v(u)=(I+Omega_v L_v(u))/2.

This is a valid projection but is not in R_v. To see exclusion without selecting a tensor basis, choose another block w. Every element of R_v commutes with Omega_w, while Gamma_v(u) anticommutes with the invertible Omega_w and is itself invertible. Thus its commutator cannot vanish.
In the factorization above E_v(u) is an effect on v together with Q, not on v alone.

One may enlarge each block algebra to include its three gamma matrices. That does not produce three independent commuting subsystem algebras: gamma_i and gamma_j in different blocks anticommute, so their spectral effects do not commute. This excludes that simultaneous independent-factor interpretation only. A single receiver allowed access to v and Q is not excluded, but that access is additional identification.
