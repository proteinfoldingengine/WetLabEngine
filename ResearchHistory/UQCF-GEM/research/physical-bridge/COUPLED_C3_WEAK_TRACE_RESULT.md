## Static certificates and graph dichotomy
Any safe static core expands each nonempty label to an original maximum. Hence static reserve existence reduces exactly to nonnegative type counts with total<=5t-1, all pair floors and at least four occupied types. Conversely an admissible maximal assignment is safe with coretau4.

An isolated vertex permits deletion of one source label at that type in4 toggles, any t>=1. Four original labels of the other types remain fixed and cover every pair root. Four is an optimal first-vacancy cost because every original label has four incidences.

When t=1, any static reserve must have exactly four active labels occupying four distinct types. The missing type must be isolated in G, since its edge to any neighbor would have cardinality1 below floor2.

For a graph on five vertices with no isolated vertex, exactly one of the following alternatives is needed (certificates may not be unique):
(A) a spanning five-cycle or a triangle plus a disjoint edge;
(B) an independent set I with |I|>|N(I)|, specifically a four-leaf star, or |I|=3 and |N(I)|=2.
They cannot coexist. A spanning five-cycle has independence number2; a triangle plus edge allows independent sets of at most2. A star has an independent four-set. A three-set as in(B) is independent.

Elementary existence proof: maximum matching has size1 or2. If size1, all graph edges intersect pairwise; such a family is a star or a triangle. A triangle cannot coexist with the remaining two nonisolated vertices without a disjoint edge, so the graph is the spanning star. If size2, choose matched edges ab,cd and unmatched vertex v. If v is adjacent to both endpoints of one matched edge, there is a triangle plus the other matched edge. Otherwise v has one or two neighbors, at most one in each matched pair. If it has neighbors a,c, and bd is an edge, v-a-b-d-c-v is a spanning five-cycle. If bd is absent, {v,b,d} is independent. If v has only neighbor a, then bc and bd cannot both exist without giving triangle bcd plus edge va. Choose c or d not adjacent to b; it and v,b form an independent three-set. Its neighborhood has size at most2 and is nonempty. Neighborhood size1 would make the remaining fifth vertex adjacent only to that neighbor (otherwise I has another neighbor), producing a spanning star, contrary to maximum matching size2. Thus it has size2.

In(A), sum the five saturated cycle inequalities with weights1/2, or the three triangle inequalities with weights1/2 plus the disjoint edge with weight1. These give sum_i n_i>=5t. No safe static reserve exists for ANY t.

In(B) with |I|3, N(I)={a,b}, assign n_i=1 on I and n_a=n_b=2t-1. Every saturated edge is between I and N or within N; all floors hold. Total4t+1<5t for t>=2. If min degree>=2 and case(B) holds, a star is impossible, and each independent vertex must meet both a and b. Thus G is K23 with optional edge ab. There are choose(5,2)*2=20 labelled graphs. ## Sharp lower bound without isolates
At any FIRST-vacancy endpoint let x be an absent label originally of type v. Since G has no isolated vertex, v has a saturated neighbor w. Root vw originally had exactly2t supporters, one of which was x. Its preserved floor2t forces an endpoint supporter y that did not originally contain vw. Thus y is distinct from x and originally has type k different from v,w. Domination forces its endpoint footprint into M_v or M_w, each intersecting its original M_k in only one root. Therefore y incurred at least three original-incidence deletions and one addition. Label x incurred at least four original-incidence deletions. These distinct-label costs sum to at least8 actual toggles. The argument allows arbitrary intermediate edits and moving covers. Thus a cherry attains the global first-vacancy optimum8 in a no-isolate graph; an isolated vertex gives optimum4. No optimality claim is made for the general <=13 route.
