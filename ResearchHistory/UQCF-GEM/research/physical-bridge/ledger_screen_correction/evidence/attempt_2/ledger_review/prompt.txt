## 1. Global preservation does not prohibit local acquisition

The information identity in section 3 compares the final full ledger with the JOINT input consisting of the initial ledger and incoming entries. It does not compare the receiver's initial ledger alone with its final ledger.

A diagnostic counterexample is a fair source bit S, an initially empty receiver ledger L0, and an incoming entry E=S. Append produces L1=[S]. Section 5 identifies a receiver output (c,r_T), not the entire recoverable ledger. With shared fixed side parameters and a source-independent readout, equal entry laws imply equal output laws.
