At the same other future inputs, retained burden may vary while the effective coefficients remain $a_{\mathrm{eff}}=a$ and $c_{\mathrm{eff}}=c$. This does not satisfy the separately stipulated exponential update for $r>0$, and it was never claimed to.
2. **Scalar classification retained with its stated codomain.** If $m:[0,\infty)\to(0,1]$, $m(0)=1$, and $m(r+s)=m(r)m(s)$, then $m(r)=\exp(-\lambda r)$ with $\lambda\ge0$. Positivity and attenuation make $g=-\log m$ additive and nonnegative, hence monotone; rational approximation gives the result without an independent continuity assumption. The identity case is $\lambda=0$.

The repair cost is zero when the base capacities are feasible for the fixed future demand. Infeasible base capacities are not an admissible counterexample instance. Capacity feasibility alone is not a proof of finite constitutive potentials.
