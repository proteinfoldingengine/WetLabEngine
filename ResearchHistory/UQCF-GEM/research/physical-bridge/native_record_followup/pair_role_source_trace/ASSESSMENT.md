## What the formulas establish, and what they do not

The product probe is explicitly

\[
\Phi_\oplus(E)(x,y)=W_B\bigl[E(x\oplus_p y)
-D_1\oplus_p[x,y](Ex)-D_2\oplus_p[x,y](Ey)\bigr].
\]

The provenance probe also uses a declared incidence action and orientation. These definitions have more content than a counts-only ledger: they retain derivative, incidence and signed-role structure. That positive content should be preserved.

However, substituting `E_event = P_e A_boundary P_e` into these probes cannot determine `P_e`; it uses `P_e`. The alternative symbol `Rep_e` is described as derived from event type, incidence and generator action, but the inspected document supplies no formula producing an owner-local representation from those inputs. Its rule against label-only component selection correctly prevents replacing this missing construction with an event name.

This is not a proof that arbitrary choices of `P_e` are native, or that no earlier/later source could constrain it. It locates the input still required by the literal recovered formula. A construction of `Rep_e` would also have to establish why its affected retained space is the same owner's observable/action space; a nonzero formal endomorphism alone would not establish an initially blind receiver's acquisition of a distinction.
