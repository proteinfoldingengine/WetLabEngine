## 2. Four endpoints are needed for an event-induced contrast

Assume, only to type the proposed comparison, that support assignments and compatible action endpoints have been supplied. Define

\[
a_X(z)=\mathcal I_X[\mathscr S_X(z)],\qquad
a_Y(Pz)=\mathcal I_Y[\mathscr S_Y(Pz)].
\]

The endpoint action increment for one preparation is

\[
J_e(z)=a_Y(Pz)-a_X(z).
\]

Its source-dependent contrast between two preparations is

\[
\Delta J_e=
a_Y(Pz_+)-a_X(z_+)-a_Y(Pz_-)+a_X(z_-).                 \tag{1}
\]

Equation (1) distinguishes two changes: applying the event, and changing the preparation. Comparing only \(a_Y(Pz_+)\) with \(a_Y(Pz_-)\) does not, in general, establish an event-created difference; the action might already have distinguished the preparations.

The established owner-coordinate calculation uses the same distinction. With \(q_p^X(z)=z_p\), \(q_p^Y(y)=y_p\), and a single pruning u into p,

\[
P^*q_p^Y=q_p^X+q_u^X,\qquad
(P^*q_p^Y-q_p^X)(z_+-z_-)=\Delta z_u.                 \tag{2}
\]

For the existing pair \((1,1,2,1)/5\) and \((1,1,1,2)/5\), the individual p-coordinate is initially blind and equation (2) equals \(1/5\). This repeats established infrastructure only to fix the comparison; it is not a new owner result. The supplied channel realization represents the post-event equality by \(C^*(\pi_p)=\pi_p+\pi_u\).

Equation (1) is a scalar action increment. It is not automatically the historical current \(\rho^\oplus\), which is intended to live in \(V_\oplus^*\), and neither is automatically an available receiver effect.

## 3. The weight-variation route gives an explicit conditional calculation

Restrict to the simplest part that V1694.4 actually specifies: fixed connection, fixed test pairs, fixed kernels and fixed norm, with only weights changing at each endpoint. Write

\[
w_X=\Lambda_X z,\quad w_Y=\Lambda_Y Pz,\quad
k_{X,a}=\|\delta_{X,a}\|^2,\quad k_{Y,b}=\|\delta_{Y,b}\|^2.
\]

The maps \(\Lambda_X,\Lambda_Y\) and the interpretation of their row indices as historical product pairs are **SUPPLIED**, not consequences of P1. Assume they give valid nonnegative weights on the stated preparation domain. Then

\[
a_X(z)=\langle k_X,\Lambda_Xz\rangle,\qquad
a_Y(Pz)=\langle k_Y,\Lambda_YPz\rangle,
\]

and elementary substitution into (1) gives

\[
\boxed{\Delta J_e=
\left\langle P^*\Lambda_Y^*k_Y-\Lambda_X^*k_X,\,
              z_+-z_-\right\rangle.}                  \tag{3}
\]

Here stars denote algebraic duals, with the usual finite-coordinate pairing; no physical metric is inferred. The V1694 weight derivative \(\delta_w\mathcal I=\sum_a\delta w_a\|\delta_a\|^2\) is exactly the derivative used in this restricted calculation.

If one proposes that this scalar action increment *equals* the owner-coordinate increment, the required relation is

\[
\left\langle
 P^*\Lambda_Y^*k_Y-\Lambda_X^*k_X-q_u^X,\,
 z_+-z_-\right\rangle=0.                              \tag{4}
\]

For a declared space V of preparation differences, equality throughout V requires the functional in (4) to annihilate V. Full equality on all formal grades is stronger and is not demanded here. Relative units must be fixed before asserting numerical equality.

Equation (4) is an obligation for this particular proposed identification, not a new universal requirement for native interaction. A different, justified relation between the current and effect could suffice. Choosing the weights to enforce (4) would assume the bridge.
