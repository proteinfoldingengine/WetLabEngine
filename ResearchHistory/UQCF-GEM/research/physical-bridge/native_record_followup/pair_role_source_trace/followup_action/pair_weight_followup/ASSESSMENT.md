\[
w_a^X(z)=\mu_z(S_a^X).
\]

The identification of S and this equation are **SUPPLIED in this sketch**; neither is asserted to follow from the inspected historical texts.

Once these meanings are granted, finite additivity already gives

\[
w_a^X(z)=\sum_{v\in S_a^X}z_v,
\qquad
\Lambda_{a v}=\mathbf 1_{\{v\in S_a^X\}}.
\]

Thus no fitted real coefficients are needed in this restricted proposal. This is an elementary consequence of the proposed meaning of weight, not a derivation of that meaning.

 =\sum_{v\in r^{-1}(S_a^Y)}z_v-\sum_{v\in S_a^X}z_v.
