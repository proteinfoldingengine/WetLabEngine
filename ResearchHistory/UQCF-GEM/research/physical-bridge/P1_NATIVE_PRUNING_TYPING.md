**Theorem 1 (typed-pruning update uniqueness).**

    mu_Y({y}) = mu_X(r_XY^{-1}({y}))
              = sum_{v:r_XY(v)=y} mu_X({v}).                 (1)

Therefore the post-event singleton-grade vector is exactly P_XY x. There is no additional selector among different grade updates once those typed inputs are granted.

For A={y}, finite additivity over the finite disjoint fiber gives (1). The retained singletons partition Y, so these values determine the entire finite additive output grade. QED.
