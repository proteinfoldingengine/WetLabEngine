Requiring the available zero-extension sections to commute too leaves only terms pairing the vertex with one of its strict ancestors. The root must have zero correction under either contract. V1712's cyclic term produces each of the six basis vectors in turn, which rules out any invertible linear reidentification that would give it the required zero root correction.
