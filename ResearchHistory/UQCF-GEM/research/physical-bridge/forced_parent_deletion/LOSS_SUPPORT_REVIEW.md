# Review of the single-deletion field component on finite rooted trees

The algebraic proof identifies every view in which the lost balanced source survives. The nonzero criterion is joint membership of the deleted site h and a nonzero signed old coefficient q. For a holder whose old source is compatible on the overlap, q is its own old subtree total. When only the passive pruning operation is enacted, the unique glued object on the preserved union stays unchanged. [General proof](LOSS_SUPPORT.md) and [the three-view example](COMMON_FIELD.md).

The proof applies to every finite rooted tree satisfying its stated premises. [Replay program](check_loss_support.py) and [results](loss_support_results.json).
