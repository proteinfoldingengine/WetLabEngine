## Single-address probes cannot supply an induced square

For a common background grade and a common amplitude \(a\ne0\), take preparation \(i\) to add \(a(e_{x_i}-e_o)\). Then the source contrast between \(i,j\) is \(a(e_{x_i}-e_{x_j})\). Its subtree contrasts are \(\pm a\) on the unique path between the endpoints and zero elsewhere. The inherited work formula gives \(a^2d_X(x_i,x_j)\), for unit edges. It is also a weighted tree distance, scaled by \(a^2\), if each edge has a fixed positive response coefficient. After P1 the site \(x_i\) is replaced by its retracted site in \(Y\), so the same form holds on every retained carrier.

Suppose the minimum-neighbor graph had a chordless four-cycle on distinct sites \(1,2,3,4\). Its four edge distances would all be the positive global minimum \(m\), and both diagonals would be strictly larger than \(m\). The three opposite-pair sums would then be \(2m,2m,D_{13}+D_{24}\), in the corresponding order. A tree metric has two equal largest opposite-pair sums. To see this directly, restrict to the subtree of the four sites. Every 1–3 split contributes equally to the three sums. Any 2–2 splits on the subtree separate the same two pairs, since two different equal-size splits are crossing and cannot both be edges of a tree. Such an edge contributes twice its positive length to two sums and zero to the third. These rules prove the equality and contradict the proposed cycle. If any sites coincide with positive amplitude, the off-diagonal minimum is zero and its graph is a union of cliques on the coincidence classes. This again has no chordless four-cycle, and the v15.43 positive-off-diagonal test also fails.

The claim covers a family of equal-amplitude single-address differences on a common background. In particular it covers transporting the single-address form of v15.40's centered sources to a tree response. It also covers arbitrary fixed positive edge lengths within that class. A four-regular nearest graph by itself is insufficient. The five leaves of a star give a complete graph on five vertices, with degree four and zero chordless squares. The original constructor must reject it at the edge-cycle test. The replay holds the fixture size at 25 for a separate single-address case and checks that it rejects at the degree test.

\[
W_{ij}=|S_i\triangle S_j|+(a_i-a_j)^2.
\]
