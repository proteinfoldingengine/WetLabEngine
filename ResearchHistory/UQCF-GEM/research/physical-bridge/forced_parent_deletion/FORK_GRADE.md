## Objects and premises

Use the two frozen source histories and paths in the fork-profile replay. Label the root 0, its left subtree 1,2,3 and its right subtree 4,5,6. Source A is index 0, receiver B index 1, and C index 2. Supply one common initial ambient rational grade g. Each view initially carries its nearest-ancestor P1 pushforward. All calculations use the original FCV Pruning.aggregate. Both positive unit probes and signed differences of two positive preparations are admitted in this formal rational space.

**Inherited mathematics.** P1 transports a deletion to its nearest retained ancestor. O2 uniquely glues compatible grades on two overlapping views. An addition in a fixed union has at least one unchanged witness for the new site. The prior routed-grade proof specifies the resulting update when the edited owner's old grade must be recovered by pruning and the updated family must agree on overlaps. The old carrier and the intersection of the witness with the new carrier cover that new carrier, so O2 applies. Each unedited grade stays fixed. Applying this mathematical lift to v16.45 incidence edits is conditional. The original controller maintains masks and its stored palette. It has no such grade argument.

## Exact comparison under continuous overlap agreement

Assume compatibility after every edit. If before the edit every view carries the projection of the same g, P1 gives its projection on a deleted view. On addition the projection of g is a valid extension, and the witness forces its uniqueness by O2. Induction fixes the family at every stage. The two paths therefore have identical grades on **all three owners** at the common full membership state for every fixed initial g. A rule reading only these grades and masks there cannot recover the earlier cover in both arms. Replacing the grade with a new branch-specific ambient input would insert the distinction to be explained.

There is a later positive result on a coordinate that B owns throughout. Both arms reach mask 17 for B with the root and bare right parent. B's fifth edit adds leaf 5 in the junction arm and removes its empty right parent in the left-leaf arm. At that edit and at completion, B's root coordinate in the left-leaf arm **minus** B's root coordinate in the junction arm is exactly

    g4 + g5 + g6.

The left-leaf arm puts the entire grade at the root. In the junction arm B still holds the right parent, so only g0+g1+g2+g3 reaches its root. Taking g to be one unit at 5 gives a root difference of +1 with a nonnegative preparation. Taking opposite grades at 5 and 6 gives zero on this particular coordinate. Pruning both B grades all the way to their common one-site view gives the identical total in both arms. Reading B's root coordinate while its larger view is available is a different operation from physically aggregating the whole view to the root. This conditional consequence follows the existing controller write and supplies no earlier selection or physical read.

## Owner-local counterexample and the exact compatibility test

Supply instead zero inclusion on each addition. This map is a mathematical right inverse to pruning, conserves total grade and leaves every old coordinate intact. Its use as the dynamical rule is an alternative premise. Initialize the three views from the same signed contrast g = delta_2 − delta_3. In the junction arm neither left leaf leaves A, and A still has +1 at 2 and −1 at 3 at the meeting. In the left-leaf arm A loses 2 and then 3 before either is restored. Both grades collapse at their common parent and sum to zero. The zero extension leaves A at zero. B and C have the same grades on the two paths at the meeting. The retained A grade now distinguishes the two prior source edits even after the masks coincide. No event flag was appended.

The control is also a difference of two nonnegative unit preparations, one at site 2 and one at 3. The left-leaf path makes their A-only grades identical after both leaf deletions. Every subsequent deterministic owner-local addition receiving the same local grade and local incidence history from that merged point must give the same result for these two probes if it has no access to prior grades or other owners. More specifically, for a deterministic memoryless owner-local lift, the post-collapse A states and continuation coincide for the two preparations. The junction path retains the original distinction at 2 under any pruning-consistent extension, since that site has a singleton fiber there. Thus a local recovery cannot reconstruct both preparations. An unchanged other owner B still holds the missing contrast during A's additions.

Along the zero-inclusion left-leaf path, the exact incompatible states are (23,15,115), (31,15,115) and (31,11,115), at path indices 3,4,5 counted with the post-source input as 0. At the first, A has just reacquired 2 at zero while B still carries +1 there. Only A and B disagree on their shared subview. When B subsequently removes its last distinguishing left leaf, both induced grades agree on the smaller overlap. Both arms are separately compatible again by (31,17,113), although they now correspond to different ambient grades. Checking only the final overlaps would therefore falsely admit an apparent history-bearing example as a continuously compatible one. This locates a specific conservation and consistency test for any proposed intrinsic memory on this episode.
