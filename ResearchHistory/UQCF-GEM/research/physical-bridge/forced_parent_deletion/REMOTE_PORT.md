# A signed source difference at one remote site and its cancellation in the shared field

Fix the four-leaf star, the same original holder C, and the same remote vertex z = v3. The archived v16.53 controller makes a second change at z with opposite signs in two histories. The inherited source pushforward turns that change into a precise signed dipole at z. The overlap term changes by exactly the same dipole. Consequently its contribution to the common field cancels, and C's own field change is zero. A separate experiment using an original command does distinguish the two saved states.

## Which signed quantity reaches the port algebraically?

Use the inherited extensive-source operator E_Y = I_YX P_XY on the ambient star X. P pushes to the nearest retained ancestor, and I extends by zero. Hold one common ambient grade g fixed. Let Y and Y' be the changing holder's views immediately before and after a single edit at z. Write s=+1 for entry and s=−1 for exit. Every vertex other than z has the same retraction in the two views. The contribution from z moves between o and z, so

    (E_Y' − E_Y)g = s g(z)(δ_z − δ_o).

At the second local edit s is positive after the v1 deletion, negative after the v4 deletion, and positive in the unedited run. The changing index is chosen by the original primitive, B in the first case and A in the second. There is also a matched comparison with the same changing index A and the same whole-path step 4 in both edited runs, with the same signs. These two comparisons use different local ordinals in the low branch. A fixed grade with g(z)=1 gives a normalized signed amplitude of +1 or −1 for this difference. It vanishes for g(z)=0. The location, sign and coefficient all follow from the original move and pushforward.

The same C view V contains z on both sides of each individual edit. Projecting the dipole to V preserves it. The unit-conductance root-zero Green inverse on V maps it to a potential with root-to-z drop s g(z). This is the field of the **difference between two representations of one old grade**. To compute the actual change of the jointly realized field one must include overlap. The v16.23 O1 identity on V and Y reads

    E_(V∪Y) = E_V + E_Y − E_(V∩Y).

The union with V stays fixed because V already contains z. The intersection gains or loses exactly z. It follows on every ambient grade that

    (E_(V∩Y') − E_(V∩Y))g = (E_Y' − E_Y)g.
    ΔE_(V∪Y) g = 0,     Δ(P_XV g) = 0.

This gives the precise cancellation of the putative port amplitude. A shared-field read, including C's own root-to-v3 difference, has zero increment from that edit. Counting the signed dipole a second time as an independent source would change the law. Over the whole invocation the positive and negative local contributions also telescope to zero because all three sequences begin and end with a single holder. The trace, additional command, component field and common field are distinct measurements.

Two further controls test the field interpretation. For a single common balanced ambient drive from o to v3, the formal root-to-v3 reading is 1 in every covering state. In fact each rooted substar has identity reduced Green matrix, and all responding views agree on shared leaves. v16.23 O4 gluing recovers the same entire ambient potential. Four independent balanced drives on every distinct state in the 2,507 original R paths give 10,304 full-field equalities over 2,576 distinct states. Linearity extends the result to every fixed balanced drive in this finite model. Separately, use a common unit count at every ambient vertex and calculate the **primary** deletion's old-minus-coarse source component from the original B view. The two drives are δ_v1−δ_o and δ_v4−δ_o. Their root-zero potentials are 1 at the corresponding deleted leaf and zero at v3, even when each component is explicitly evaluated as a drive. Both differ from the signed secondary edit at v3. Neither calculation introduces a physical time coordinate or physical units.
