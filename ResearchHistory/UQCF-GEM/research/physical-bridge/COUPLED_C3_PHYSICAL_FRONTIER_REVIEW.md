## 1. What the inspected documents actually say

The native-response gate does **not** require determinism as a universal physical axiom. Its output obligation explicitly allows a probabilistic alternative with a separately frozen record/error contract. Its sixth obligation separates conditional response, enforcement, realized outcome, and guaranteed progress. It also declares that the restored-marker interface is one narrow consumer, not the only possible physical response. The rejected symmetry argument correctly distinguishes a deterministic selector from an invariant probability distribution, and correctly withdraws its no-go once supplied palette order is restored.

Accordingly, it would be inaccurate to report that these documents prove stochastic mechanisms impossible or literally impose deterministic selection. Their immediate candidate stops are earlier and narrower: no derived carrier/action/access connection was found. The problem is a possible interpretation of their repeated OPEN labels: reading “actual selection remains open” as “nothing physically useful can be established until a unique actual outcome is predicted” would add a requirement the evidence does not justify.

## 2. The consequential distinctions

**A law need not predict a unique outcome from the declared initial input.** A derived stochastic interaction with an earned accessible record channel could be real progress without a deterministic selector. Equivariance of a law does not imply pointwise fixed outcomes. Conversely, merely displaying an invariant distribution over legal commands supplies neither physical interaction nor record production. Adding that distribution by hand is particularly disallowed here. A proof must derive whatever stochastic structure it uses from already admitted premises; this review introduces none.

**A supplied record value and a generated record channel are different.** In v15.11, select_record demands a record integer from its caller. This is positive evidence that this particular function does not generate the record. It is not a theorem that a native interaction must compute that integer deterministically. The same file explicitly demonstrates different instruments with the same nonselective dephasing channel. That identifies an instrument-level underdetermination, not an impossibility of deriving an instrument from additional existing interaction structure.

**A generated record correlation and unique global actuality are also different.** Deriving an action that establishes and retains a system/reference correlation would address a missing physical connection even if a separate unique-actuality interpretation remained open. A joint coherent state or ensemble expectation alone must not be relabelled as an acquired single actual record. The correct report would name the new correlation/retention result and preserve its remaining actual-record premise, rather than either declaring full closure or dismissing the entire advance.

**Progress and guaranteed termination are different.** An admissible toggle-or-NOOP relation allows an all-NOOP history, so that relation alone cannot imply guaranteed finite completion. It does not rule out an independently derived law with a justified progress guarantee of some other scope. Neither positive success probability nor eventual success follows without that law and the relevant assumptions. No fairness, retry independence, positive rate, or hidden-dependent transition probabilities may be inserted to fill the gap.

**Physical interaction is not an observer's full-state read.** The absence of an accessible support snapshot does not alone prove that an existing system/reference coupling cannot be support-sensitive. An observer need not reconstruct every variable that an interaction physically involves. But invoking “the system knows its own state” is not a derivation. Here the coupling must come from existing native operations; an evaluator inspecting hidden support and then generating a convenient output or outcome kernel still fails the user's constraint. This distinction identifies the search target without relaxing the constraint.

In particular, any effective input-dependent output statistics must be consequences of the earned interaction, not its postulated replacement. Giving a hidden-state evaluator a physical-sounding name does not change its status. The evidence required is the existing operation's derivation and data flow through the system/reference carrier, including why the observer can access the output without accessing the hidden input. None is supplied here.

## 3. Full erasure and unique minimal pruning are unjustified requirements

Neither record origin nor the stated time-as-pruning position requires erasing all unused information, selecting the most-forgetful channel, or choosing the least sufficient retained set on every update. Record formation normally needs some distinctions to survive. If a proposed step is claimed to constitute strict pruning, it must demonstrate the specific loss of distinction under its earned retained-description map. Identity alone cannot demonstrate that claim. That does not make identity invalid as a sufficient-retention control, and it does not require maximal loss.

The lineage result correctly proves that the sufficient prefix-closed sets form the interval from Y_min to X. It correctly refuses to turn leastness into an actual pruning rule. This is underdetermination under preservation constraints, not a general impossibility of physical pruning. The v15.22 identity-only result likewise concerns the stated open two-event, all-input, full-branch-output preservation contract. It cannot be exported to every finite record task, restricted source class, or physically earned observable family. Relaxing that mathematical contract is not itself physical progress; the weaker contract must be justified by a real native consumer rather than chosen just to permit deletion.

The v15.11 executable source additionally distinguishes retained distinguishability loss from reversibility in a supplied full coherent dilation, and warns that the dilation is only a mathematical control. Thus neither global irreversibility nor a new physical environment is earned by the displayed channel. Preserve time-as-pruning at the demonstrated distinction-loss/compatible-record-order level; do not insert a time parameter, rate, duration, metric, or geometry to animate it.

## 4. The precise corrected next obligation

The next construction obligation should be:

> From explicitly identified existing native operations and retained/reference data, derive one nontrivial action-to-accessible-record coupling whose output records a physical relative distinction, while exposing every supplied premise. Demonstrate its carrier binding, allowed update semantics, and retention/access. State separately whether it establishes a conditional correlation, a stochastic record law, a realized-record account, or a progress guarantee.

This is a construction obligation, not permission to add an interaction primitive. A useful submission must name the actual existing operation, its domain and codomain, and the already available source/reference inputs; a newly named abstract coupling would not satisfy it.

For the currently chosen C3 marker consumer, the smallest decisive proof target is concrete:

1. Start with the inherited two swapped-support cases and the same declared accessible input/reference preparation. Identify, using native structure, how the fixed named request engages the appropriate root in each case. Do not initialize the record with the hidden support answer or choose a cross-carrier dictionary.
2. Derive the system/reference update and the actual retained output coordinates from that operation. Include all permitted outcomes, including NOOP where applicable. An executable verifier can independently check safety but cannot be substituted for physical enforcement or observer access.
3. Prove the named output distinction against a fixed reference. Under the existing exact contract, the output must meet the inherited singleton/pair increment rule for the actual marker update, with NOOP distinguished as specified. If an already derived stochastic channel cannot meet that exact contract, type its accessible statistical record contract separately before evaluation; establish an actual difference of accessible output laws or records. A difference of hidden branch labels or an unobservable expectation is insufficient. Do not demand disjoint outcome supports unless exact single-event discrimination is the chosen claim.
4. Show which of these record distinctions survive the candidate's earned retention operation, and show a strict lost distinction if this step is claimed to produce pruning-time. Keep selection, actual-record interpretation, and progress as separately tracked results. A proof of only the first three items is a conditional physical subbridge, not full native closure, but is still a new connection if the interaction itself was genuinely derived.

The existing gate already contains most of this structure. The methodological correction is to treat a derived coupling as an independently valuable milestone, not to make deterministic resolution or universal completion its admission condition. Conversely, an arbitrary instrument attached to a blank register is another supplied conditional model and does not meet the construction obligation.

The gate's source-event-to-C3 binding is necessary for its direct reuse of J_s; it is not proved necessary for every native interaction route. If another existing operation could couple the C3 carrier directly to an earned reference, requiring it first to pass through J_s would impose unnecessary architecture. No such alternative operation was found in the inspected material, and this review does not posit one.

## 6. Adjudication

**Material correction to research direction:** stop using “no selected actual record yet” as a universal blocker on recognizing a newly derived native interaction/retention subbridge. Demand the native coupling first, and then classify its actuality and progress scope honestly. Do not require deterministic uniqueness, full erasure, maximum pruning, or guaranteed success on every legal request.

**No reversal of the inspected candidate verdicts:** their missing source/reference/action/access connections are substantive. Replacing deterministic selection language with probabilistic language does not repair them. A hidden-dependent outcome kernel, sampled legal command, or forced-commit rule remains inadmissible unless its relevant law is already derived; no such derivation is provided here.

**What is new in this review:** a more precise ordering and separation of proof obligations, including the distinction between an inaccessible verifier and a physically derived coupling, and between strict pruning and maximal erasure. This is a conceptual audit, not a new physical mechanism, newly earned instrument, no-go theorem, numerical result, or closure claim. No archive-wide impossibility is asserted. The physical record-origin construction remains open in the inspected evidence.
