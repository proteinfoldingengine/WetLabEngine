## 1. Correct the target before adding a postulate

The existing nonempty nested-support grammar already admits a receiver deletion at a covered leaf and excludes the corresponding deletion at an uncovered leaf. The most recent discussion correctly separates this grammar from the larger family of linear pushforwards. It is not necessary to invent a second same-union law if an actual event can be derived to belong to this existing grammar.

The remaining question is whether an independently specified native event on independently identified owned records has that interpretation. A definition of a legal incidence edit does not identify a physical occurrence with one. Conversely, a legitimate source-dependent joint interaction is not an illicit observer-side read merely because its action depends on the source.

The proposed bridge below separates ownership, event realization, occurrence and readable retention. No claim is made that these are logically independent axioms, minimal assumptions, or the only possible realization. In particular, one should not describe all the outstanding work as a single missing transition law.

## 2. Keep the mathematical target fixed

Use the eight-vertex rooted tree T with two- and three-leaf branches, source label b and receiver label r. The same extensive grade is x=1_T in both preparations. The initial source extent is B0={o,c_s}; the receiver extent is T. The compatible joint record is (P_TB0 x,x), with source payload (5,3) or (4,4), while the complete declared receiver record (T,x) is identical.

The candidate source update has B=B0 union H, with nonempty H contained in L_s, and source payload P_TB x; the receiver record stays (T,x). The downstream candidate is the inherited map

    Q_l(y_b,y_r) = (y_b, P_T,T_l y_r),  T_l=T minus {l}.

The accepted construction establishes that its cover-compatible first receiver deletions have l in H. It does not select a leaf or require a normalized probability distribution over the five overlapping quantum complement effects. The extensive grade remains distinct from a density matrix.

## 3. Proposed realization clauses, each with an evidence obligation

**O — Owned state association.** Identify actual native configurations omega_s and the same persistent source and receiver, with an independently defined interpretation pi into the above compatible records. The complete actual receiver restrictions must agree across s initially. Equality only of the displayed grade or a chosen observable is insufficient. The map pi must be defined from native records before looking at whether the decoder succeeds, and must respect the inherited structural identifications. Names b,r, a fiber product, and an arbitrary tensor product do not establish ownership.

**S — Source update.** Identify an actual native operation E_b on those same configurations. It must produce the indicated nonempty branch-confined H and its compatible payload while leaving the complete receiver restriction unchanged. The existing compatible-record argument fixes the payload once the extent is earned. It does not create the extent. If a record-algebra refinement is used, derive that its current central-record data represent this same owned b extent; do not change from an actual-history interpretation for b to an incompatible catalogue interpretation for r without justification. The established F(C,h) representation is a candidate association, not its native proof.

**E — Actual-event typing.** Identify an actual receiver operation E on the post-source native configuration, and derive a commuting relation

    pi(E(omega)) = Q_l(pi(omega))

where the incidence component of E is a legal primitive of the existing fixed-carrier grammar. This is a proposed event-realization requirement, NOT a definition of E by taking all and only the Q_l that decode correctly. No converse is required: not every abstractly legal edit need occur. The label l may be nonunique. A stochastic realization must specify its event space and law independently; neither equal weights nor a particular leaf selector is presumed.

The required proof must show where actual-event typing comes from. It cannot infer it from pushforward composition, positivity, grade conservation, fixed-record truth or the fact that joint reconstruction works. Those routes have already been checked. Nor should it add all-input joint injectivity as an unexplained physical axiom: the existing grammar could instead earn the admissible domain through an actual realization.

**A — Available retained output.** Derive that the resulting receiver-owned record provides the rooted extent information needed for the accepted shape readout, and survives until a justified receiver use. This need not grant complete access to a shared quantum algebra or introduce a copied register. Access only to the total grade, cardinality, or unordered grade histogram is insufficient in this example. A later restoration can erase the distinction; no indefinite memory is assumed.

**N — Nonvacuous occurrence.** For an actual-case claim, exhibit a realized event and retained receiver use. For a probabilistic law claim, derive a positive probability of the specified retained outcome in the independently defined trial class. Mere nonemptiness of H or abstract existence of Q_l is insufficient. There is no universal-progress, fairness, deterministic-selection or fundamental-time requirement. Event ordering here is the inherited order of retained updates.

These clauses are a precise bridge specification. Until O and an independently specified event supply their native meaning, they are not an independently motivated physical mechanism or an executable experiment. Their function is to make a proposed extension challengeable without presenting its desired conclusion as a derivation.

## 4. Existing conditional consequence and how a realization would be challenged

The already-reviewed first-response consequence is:

| Preparation | Initial receiver record | Record after its first admitted deletion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| b marks the two-leaf branch | (T,1_T), branch degrees {2,3} | seven vertices, branch degrees {1,3} |
| b marks the three-leaf branch | the same (T,1_T) | seven vertices, branch degrees {2,2} |

Both output grade histograms are one 2 and six 1s; both totals are 8. The structural distinction, not the histogram, carries the conditional answer. These are inherited analytic consequences, not new measured results.

After the native association and event class are fixed independently, the following are substantive challenges to this proposed application:

- A branch-confined source update that changes the complete receiver restriction already during E_b defeats the claimed initially blind source-stage preservation.
- A first receiver event outside H, within the independently specified episode and with the claimed carrier and source state unchanged, defeats E's asserted typing. Do not exclude it afterward by defining a successful event as one in H.
- A post-event physical record that cannot support the declared structural distinction defeats A even when an external diagnostic can reconstruct the tree.
- A record restored to the blank before receiver use supplies no retained acquisition. Do not treat an externally archived intermediate image as proof that the receiver used it.
- If a positive response probability is claimed, all admitted trials, including blanks and failures, must be accounted for under a prospectively specified trial and sampling contract. This proposal fixes no rate or lower bound; absence of responses in a finite sample alone cannot refute an unspecified positive probability.

The terminal shapes provide a conditional discriminating signature. They become a physical prediction only after independently justified preparation, event, readout and trial associations exist. We cannot presently name an apparatus, response rate or physical units from these premises. Simulating the incidence grammar would demonstrate the supplied model, not establish those associations.
