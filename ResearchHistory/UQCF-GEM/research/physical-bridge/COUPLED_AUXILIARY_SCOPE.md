## Preliminary structural gate to prove before choosing a carrier

For n nonempty roots:
    tau=n iff supports are pairwise disjoint.

Consequences to prove:
- n=3 and tau>=3 forbids any overlap.
- n=4 with overlap and 3<=tau<=4 forces tau=3.

This fixes the minimal root count for an overlapping target-four carrier at n=4, and rules out misleading three-root overlap claims.
