## Required mathematical bridge, now typed

These are acceptance obligations for a future candidate, not definitions of a newly earned mechanism.

Let X be the C3 incidence-state domain, A the named marker requests, and T_a the declared resolved transition relation (including NOOP). Let Q be a previously constructed retained-state domain with its previously justified request/update semantics.

A candidate must establish all of the following from native inputs:

1. **Carrier connection.** Give a justified map or relation between X and Q, with its accessible initialization data stated. A full-state encoding chosen to store each hidden support in an orthogonal label does not establish an observer-accessible initialization.
2. **Action connection.** Show that the existing Q update associated with the named request realizes the corresponding C3 addition/removal relation. For a deterministic encoding F and an actual C3 successor E', the proposed Q successor must represent F(E'). NOOP must be represented separately. A set of possible successors still does not select an actual one.
3. **Retained response.** Identify previously available retained outputs and derive an exact decoder for the marker-star increment from them. For an actual fresh addition, its singleton coordinate is -1 and its pair coordinate at y is 0 for y in the selected old support, -1 otherwise; NOOP gives zero. Merely defining those values from the hidden support does not produce the channel.
4. **Accessible record.** Explain how the actual response becomes retained and available to the controller across ordered slices, including the before/after association. Preserve the existing RAS/RCR premises unless an independent construction removes them.
5. **Scope.** Distinguish system-side state inputs from observer-side records, and exact responses from statistical estimators. State freshness, addressing, exclusivity and completion premises. No progress law follows from a legal transition relation.

For probabilistic Q descriptions, an ensemble expectation identity alone cannot satisfy item 4. A proposed probabilistic alternative must state its own record and error contract; it cannot silently replace the exact channel used by the closed probe.

The minimum discriminator is already available and must not be republished as a new counterexample: use the closed swapped-root pair (triangle core, residual supports {d,e,u} and {d,e,v} exchanged between named slots). The initial aggregate miss fields agree, while fresh +w(r0) has different {w,u} increments. A candidate must show where the justified action/retained-output connection distinguishes these histories. Assigning their answers by hand fails the origin question.

## Decision and stopping boundary

This stops the proposed shortcut of treating the earlier quantum-response output or ensemble decoder as an already derived marker sensor. It is a source-scoped failed route assessment, not an archive-wide absence proof, a universal no-go theorem, or a newly closed checkpoint.
