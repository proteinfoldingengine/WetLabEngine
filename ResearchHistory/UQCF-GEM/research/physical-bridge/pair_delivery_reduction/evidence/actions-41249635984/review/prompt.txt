## 31. Shape-uniformity is used only as sibling exchange at the climb's own forks: with address-dependent reads, every climb unit is read faithfully (new in draft 25)

Where this sits. Section 30 left one assumption beyond the inherited class: one read rule across the comb's rooted subtrees, that is, isomorphism invariance of the reads between different carriers. If a read law is by definition a function of the carrier up to rooted isomorphism, that holds automatically and section 30 applies as it stands. This section treats the weaker case in which the read law may depend on lineage addresses, so that each lineage may have its own rule, and asks how much uniformity the construction actually uses. (a) A single realized pruning history cannot supply it. Its carriers strictly decrease in size, so no two are isomorphic, and along one history a leaf may hold any fraction t ∈ [0, κ] of its receiver's unit at every step (Remark 31.4). (b) On the address class, (PN) still reduces the reads to one table, now indexed by addresses (Lemma 31.0). (c) (PN), (POS) and blindness along one addressed climb clean every side branch off the climb. The only freedom left in the climb's columns is a bottleneck leak t_m through the climb's own next vertex, which bounds every descendant's share of the unit (Theorem 31.1; [D26]). (d) Exchange symmetry of the two children of each climb vertex, on one fork carrier per depth (D automorphisms of single carriers in all), removes that leak. Every climb unit is then read faithfully, F̂_W e_{a_m} = κ e_{a_m}, on every carrier that contains it, whatever else the carrier contains (Theorem 31.2). So for steps above the horizon the transfer is context-independent (Corollary 31.3). The transfer is the same as before. At the step that prunes a_d, the receiver a_{d−1} gains κ g, which is exactly the leaf's holding, and the total holding is conserved (Lemma 27.0).

**Setting.** 𝒜 is a rooted tree of lineage addresses with Genesis 0 as its root. Assume each a_m with m ≤ D − 1 has a second child address u_m ≠ a_{m+1}, and let F_m = [0, a_m] ∪ {a_{m+1}, u_m} be the fork carrier. (SX_m), sibling exchange on F_m, is F̂_{F_m} σ_m = σ_m F̂_{F_m}, where σ_m swaps a_{m+1} and u_m. This is invariance under one automorphism of one carrier, not a comparison of two carriers. Notation: v ∧ z is the deepest common ancestor of v and z. For w an ancestor-or-self of v, A_v(w) = Σ_{w′ ≤ w} ν_v(w′), the sum running over the ancestors-or-self w′ of w. κ := A_0(0).

**Lemma 31.0 (address table).** (PN) holds on the address class if and only if there are numbers ν_v(w), one for each address v and each ancestor-or-self w of v and independent of the carrier, such that s_v = Σ_{w ≤ v} ν_v(w) c_w on every carrier containing v. The holding of v under the unit grade e_z is then h_v(e_z) = A_v(v ∧ z) − Σ_{u ∈ ch_W(v)} A_u(u ∧ z).
*Proof.* This is Lemma 29.0 without its last use of isomorphism invariance. Write s_v = Σ_w M_W(v, w) c_w. Deleting leaves down to the subtree spanned by the paths to v and w gives M_W(v, w) = 0 unless w ≤ v, and M_W(v, w) = M_{[0,v]}(v, w) otherwise. For the holding formula, s_v(e_z) = Σ_{w ≤ v, w ≤ z} ν_v(w) = A_v(v ∧ z) and F̂(x)_v = s_v − Σ_{children} s_u. The converse is the computation of Proposition 26.1. QED.

**Theorem 31.1 (without uniformity, the side branches are clean and the climb's next vertex may leak).** Assume (PN), (POS) and (Bl_D^a) on the address class. Then:
(a) A_{a_d}(a_d) = κ ≥ 0 for 0 ≤ d ≤ D.
(b) *Side branches are clean.* For m ≤ D − 1, every child u ≠ a_{m+1} of a_m and every descendant c of u (u included), A_c(a_m) = 0.
(c) *The only freedom is along the climb.* For m ≤ D − 1 and every descendant c of a_{m+1} with parent c′ ≠ a_m, 0 ≤ A_c(a_m) ≤ A_{c′}(a_m). Hence 0 ≤ A_c(a_m) ≤ t_m := A_{a_{m+1}}(a_m) ≤ κ.
(d) *Every leak occurs.* For 0 < κ, m ≤ D − 1 and t ∈ [0, κ], let A_v(w) = κ [w = v] (the κ id table), except A_{a_{m+1}}(a_m) = t and A_c(c) = κ − t for every c strictly below a_m and not at or below a_{m+1}. This table satisfies (PN), (POS) and (Bl_D^a), with acquisition κ g at every climb step. On the chain [0, a_{m+1}], the leaf a_{m+1} reads t x_{a_m}, so (E0) fails at the climb's own next vertex when t > 0. On F_m, a_{m+1} holds t of e_{a_m} while u_m holds 0, so (SX_m) fails for t > 0.
*Proof.* (a) This is Step 1 of Theorem 29.1. On [0, a_d], h_{a_{d−1}}(e_{a_d}) = A_{a_{d−1}}(a_{d−1}) − A_{a_d}(a_d), and (POS) on {0} gives κ ≥ 0. (b) On the fork [0, a_m] ∪ {a_{m+1}, u} with x = e_{a_{m+1}}, the vertex u holds A_u(a_m), and a_m holds A_{a_m}(a_m) − A_{a_{m+1}}(a_{m+1}) − A_u(a_m) = −A_u(a_m) by (a), since m + 1 ≤ D. (POS) at both gives A_u(a_m) = 0. For descendants, on the chain [0, c] with x = e_{a_m}, the parent c′ holds A_{c′}(a_m) − A_c(a_m) and c holds A_c(a_m). So 0 ≤ A_c(a_m) ≤ A_{c′}(a_m), and induction from u gives 0. (c) The same chain inequality, with induction from a_{m+1}. On [0, a_{m+1}] with x = e_{a_m}, a_m holds κ − t_m ≥ 0, and a_{m+1} holds t_m ≥ 0. (d) Only the holdings of a_m, of a_{m+1} and of the vertices in the side subtrees of a_m change. For v in a side subtree, both A_v(v ∧ z) and the children's entries are (κ − t) times the corresponding indicator, so h_v(e_z) = (κ − t)[z = v] ≥ 0. For v = a_{m+1}, the children u satisfy A_u(a_m) = 0, so h_v(e_z) = κ [z = a_{m+1}] + t [a_{m+1} ∧ z = a_m] ≥ 0. For v = a_m: if z = a_m, h = κ − t [a_{m+1} ∈ W] ≥ 0. If z lies at or below a_{m+1}, h = κ − κ = 0. If z lies in a side subtree, h = κ − (κ − t) − t [a_{m+1} ∈ W] ≥ 0. (Bl_D^a) holds because the climb's diagonal entries are unchanged, (PN) holds by Lemma 31.0, and the gain at every step is the leaf's holding κ (Lemma 27.0). On F_m with x = e_{a_m}: h_{a_{m+1}} = t and h_{u_m} = A_{u_m}(a_m) = 0. QED.

**Theorem 31.2 (sibling exchange at the climb's forks makes every climb unit faithful).** Assume (PN), (POS), (Bl_D^a) and (SX_m) for 0 ≤ m ≤ D − 1. Then t_m = 0 and A_{u_m}(u_m) = κ for every m ≤ D − 1, and on every carrier W that contains a_m (m ≤ D − 1),

*Proof.* σ_m fixes a_m. So (SX_m) applied to x = e_{a_m} gives h_{a_{m+1}}(e_{a_m}) = h_{u_m}(e_{a_m}) on F_m, that is, t_m = A_{u_m}(a_m) = 0 by Theorem 31.1(b). Then A_c(a_m) = 0 for every c strictly below a_m, by Theorem 31.1(b)-(c). Applied to x = e_{a_{m+1}}, (SX_m) gives A_{u_m}(u_m) = h_{u_m}(e_{u_m}) = h_{a_{m+1}}(e_{a_{m+1}}) = κ. **Corollary 31.3 (a context-independent transfer above the horizon).** Under the premises of Theorem 31.2, let 1 ≤ d ≤ D − 1, and let W be any carrier in which a_d is a leaf with parent a_{d−1}. Prune a_d (the P1 step, Y = W − a_d). Before the step, F̂_W(g e_{a_d}) = κ g e_{a_d}: the receiver a_{d−1} and every other vertex read nothing of the distinction, and only its own address holds it. After the step, F̂_Y(P g e_{a_d}) = F̂_Y(g e_{a_{d−1}}) = κ g e_{a_{d−1}}: the receiver holds exactly κ g, and every other vertex still holds nothing of it. The total holding κ g is conserved. This holds in every carrier containing the climb's lineage, not only on the chain where blindness was imposed. For d = D, the before-step statement holds on the chain [0, a_D] by (Bl_D^a), but not in every context, because column a_D is not controlled (horizon).
*Proof.* Theorem 31.2 at m = d and at m = d − 1, together with P e_{a_d} = e_{a_{d−1}} (CN). QED.

**Remark 31.4 (one realized history supplies no uniformity).** Let W_0 ⊃ W_1 ⊃ … be a realized pruning history, one leaf per step. Sizes strictly decrease, so no two of its carriers are isomorphic, and invariance between isomorphic carriers says nothing inside it. Suppose the maps on the history's carriers are otherwise free. Then (PN) at the step from W_i to W_{i+1} (leaf l, receiver p) determines every row of F̂_{W_i} except that the l-row and the p-row are fixed only in their sum. If F̂_{W_{i+1}} = κ id, then (POS) and the receiver's blindness leave exactly F̂_{W_i} = κ id + t (E_{lp} − E_{pp}) with t ∈ [0, κ]: the leaf holds t of its receiver's unit. So a history's own steps cannot decide (E0). The comparison has to come from other configurations in the class (sections 29-30) or from symmetry within one carrier (this section).
*Proof.* Put φ(x) = (F̂_{W_i} x)_l and ψ(x) = (F̂_{W_{i+1}} P x)_p. Then (F̂_{W_i} x)_p = ψ(x) − φ(x), and every other row is a row of F̂_{W_{i+1}} P. (POS) gives 0 ≤ φ(e_v) ≤ ψ(e_v) = κ [v ∈ {p, l}]. Blindness gives φ(e_l) = ψ(e_l) = κ. Only φ(e_p) = t ∈ [0, κ] remains free. QED.

Reading. Section 30 said that what the positivity route needs beyond the inherited class is one read rule across the comb's rooted subtrees. This section shows that even this is more than is used. Let the read law depend on lineage addresses. Positivity, the ledger and one blind climb already clean every lineage that branches off the climb. This is the same sandwich as in section 29: the blind receiver and its empty side child together hold zero of the next climb unit. What remains is one bottleneck number per depth, t_m, the share of a climb vertex's unit held by the climb's own next vertex; vertices below it may share part of t_m, never more ([D26](iii)). This is the one place where the empty-child comparison cannot reach, because the next vertex is not empty there. A single symmetry of a single carrier, exchanging the climb's next vertex with its empty sibling, sets t_m = 0. With those D exchanges, every climb unit is held only at its own address in every carrier, and the receiver of every step above the horizon is blind before and gains exactly κ g after, whatever else is present. The symmetry used is the structural indistinguishability of two siblings within one configuration. It is weaker than requiring two different configurations to be read by the same rule.

## 33. The event transfer is two linear equalities per lineage: an uninformed receiver and an off-lineage reader that reads nothing at its departure point (new in draft 28)

Where this sits. [D27](i) showed that the step that actually occurs needs neither (SE) nor the climb's full columns: (PN), (Bl_D^a) and (POS_𝒯) already give it. This section asks what the step needs exactly. Under (PN) alone, with no positivity, no sibling comparison and no uniformity between carriers, the all-context event transfer is equivalent to two families of linear equalities. The first is the uninformed receiver along the climb, (Bl_D^a). The second, (Side_D), is a restricted form of cone-locality. Each address that leaves the record's lineage reads nothing of the unit at the vertex where it leaves. Every equality is needed, and nothing else in the table is constrained. Positivity on the climb's forks and lines of descent (Theorem 31.1(b)) and cone-local reads (S) of section 26 are two different sources of (Side_D). Neither is part of the transfer's content. The ledger point is visible directly: the total holding of every unit is κ on every carrier, whatever the table, so the transfer only moves the unit's holding from the leaf to the receiver.

**Setting.** As in section 31: the address class of finite rooted subtrees W of 𝒜, one linear read map per carrier, (PN) on every leaf pruning, the address table A_v(w) of Lemma 31.0, κ := A_0(0), and the climb a_0 = 0, …, a_D. No (POS), no (SE_m) or (SX_m), and no invariance between carriers. For an address c that is not on the climb and not at or below a_D, its departure point is δ(c) := c ∧ a_D = a_i with i ≤ D − 1; c lies at or below a child u ≠ a_{i+1} of a_i. A climb vertex with no second child address has no such c. Conditions:
- (ET_D) *Event transfer in every context.* For 1 ≤ d ≤ D and every carrier W in which a_d is a leaf, F̂_W e_{a_d} = κ e_{a_d}.
- (Diag_D) A_{a_d}(a_d) = κ for 1 ≤ d ≤ D.
- (Side_D) A_c(δ(c)) = 0 for every address c that leaves the climb at some a_i with i ≤ D − 1.

**Lemma 33.0 (ledger conservation for every table).** Under (PN), Σ_{v ∈ W} h_v(e_z) = κ for every carrier W and every z ∈ W.
*Proof.* By Lemma 31.0, Σ_v h_v(e_z) = Σ_v A_v(v ∧ z) − Σ_{v ≠ 0} A_v(v ∧ z) = A_0(0) = κ, because every nonroot vertex is the child of exactly one vertex of W. QED.

**Theorem 33.1 (the exact content of the event transfer).** Under (PN) on the address class, the following are equivalent:
(i) (ET_D);
(ii) (Bl_D^a) and (Side_D);
(iii) (Diag_D) and (Side_D).
Under any of them, for every carrier W in which a_d is a leaf (1 ≤ d ≤ D) and Y = W − a_d, also F̂_Y e_{a_{d−1}} = κ e_{a_{d−1}}: before the P1 step only the leaf holds the distinction g e_{a_d}, in the amount κ g, and after it only the receiver a_{d−1} does, in the same amount.
*Proof.* (ii) ⇔ (iii): on the chain [0, a_d], the receiver a_{d−1} holds h_{a_{d−1}}(e_{a_d}) = A_{a_{d−1}}(a_{d−1}) − A_{a_d}(a_d), so blindness at the d-th step is A_{a_d}(a_d) = A_{a_{d−1}}(a_{d−1}), and the D steps together are (Diag_D). This is the first step of Theorem 31.1(a), which does not use (POS); (POS) there gave only κ ≥ 0.
(iii) ⇒ (i): let a_d be a leaf of W. Then W contains no vertex at or below a child of a_d, so every v ∈ W is either a climb vertex a_i with i ≤ d or lies at or below a child u ≠ a_{i+1} of some a_i with i ≤ d − 1, and then v ∧ a_d = a_i = δ(v). The leaf a_d holds A_{a_d}(a_d) = κ. A proper ancestor a_i holds A_{a_i}(a_i) − A_{a_{i+1}}(a_{i+1}) − Σ_u A_u(a_i) = κ − κ − 0, the sum over its other children u in W, each with δ(u) = a_i. A vertex v off the climb holds A_v(a_i) − Σ_{u ∈ ch_W(v)} A_u(a_i), and v and its children all have departure point a_i, so every term is zero by (Side_D).
(i) ⇒ (iii): on [0, a_d], (ET_D) gives h_{a_{d−1}}(e_{a_d}) = 0, which is (Diag_D) as above. For (Side_D), let c leave the climb at a_i, i ≤ D − 1, and put d = i + 1 and W = [0, a_d] ∪ [0, c]. This is a carrier, a_d is a leaf of it (c does not lie below a_d), and c is a leaf of it. So h_c(e_{a_d}) = A_c(c ∧ a_d) = A_c(a_i) = 0.
After the step: (PN) gives F̂_Y P = P F̂_W on the step that prunes a_d, and P e_{a_d} = e_{a_{d−1}} (CN), so F̂_Y e_{a_{d−1}} = P(κ e_{a_d}) = κ e_{a_{d−1}}. QED.

**Proposition 33.2 (each equality is needed, and nothing else is constrained).**
(a) *Independence.* Start from the κ id table with κ ≠ 0. Changing one entry A_c(δ(c)) of (Side_D) to ε ≠ 0, or one diagonal entry A_{a_d}(a_d) to κ + ε, leaves every other equality of (Diag_D) and (Side_D) true and makes (ET_D) false (on [0, a_{i+1}] ∪ [0, c], respectively on [0, a_d]). So the system has exactly D + |{c : c leaves the climb at some a_i, i ≤ D − 1}| independent equalities, one per listed entry.
(b) *No positivity.* Every table entry outside (Diag_D) and (Side_D) is free, over Q or R, signed or not, including the climb's next-vertex leaks t_m = A_{a_{m+1}}(a_m), every entry A_c(a_j) with c ∧ a_D strictly below a_j, every non-climb diagonal and every entry of an address at or below a_D. For example, the κ id table with t_m = t for any real t satisfies (ET_D). It satisfies (POS) only for t = 0 when a_m has a second child: with x = e_u for a second child u of a_m, the vertex a_m holds κ − t − κ = −t on [0, a_{m+1}] ∪ {u}, and t < 0 makes a_{m+1} negative on [0, a_{m+1}]. Theorem 31.1(d) needs the side subtrees rescaled to κ − t for that reason. It satisfies (FC) only for t = 0.
(c) *No second child is needed.* If a climb vertex a_i has no second child address in 𝒜, no address leaves the climb at a_i, and (Side_D) imposes nothing there. The premise "a second child address at each climb vertex above the horizon", used in [D27](i) for the fork rows, is needed for that source of (Side_D), not for the transfer. On a chain 𝒜, (ET_D) is (Bl_D^a).
(d) *Sign of κ.* (ET_D) holds for every real κ. Acquisition (a nonzero gain κ g) needs κ ≠ 0; κ > 0 needs a separate condition, for example (POS) on {0}.

## P0 — Scalar structure and linearity

## P1 — Admissibility, fiber composition and cumulative kernels

## 2. Theorem P (pair delivery from sibling exchange) — PROVED

#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""T-235 producer: pair-delivery premise reduction (exact, sympy over Q).

Enumeration: rooted unlabeled trees by recursive multiset partitions of child subtrees.
Linear algebra: sympy nullspaces of stacked constraint matrices.
Writes a canonical JSON certificate to stdout (deterministic bytes)."""
import hashlib
import json
import sys
from fractions import Fraction
from itertools import combinations_with_replacement

import sympy as sp

# ---------- trees as nested sorted tuples ----------
def trees(n, memo={}):
    """All rooted unlabeled trees with n vertices, as canonical nested tuples."""
    if n in memo:
        return memo[n]
    if n == 1:
        memo[1] = [()]
        return memo[1]
    out = set()
    def forests(m, maxkey):
        if m == 0:
            yield ()
            return
        for k in range(1, m + 1):
            for t in trees(k):
                key = (k, t)
                if maxkey is not None and key > maxkey:
                    continue
                for rest in forests(m - k, key):
                    yield (t,) + rest
    for f in forests(n - 1, None):
        out.add(tuple(sorted(f)))
    memo[n] = sorted(out)
    return memo[n]

def size(t):
    return 1 + sum(size(c) for c in t)

def materialize(t):
    """Vertices as address tuples, preorder; returns list of addresses."""
    out = []
    def rec(node, addr):
        out.append(addr)
        for i, c in enumerate(node):
            rec(c, addr + (i,))
    rec(t, ())
    return out

def subtree_at(t, addr):
    for i in addr:
        t = t[i]
    return t

def canon_with_marks(t, marks):
    """Canonical string of tree with some addresses marked (for orbit identification)."""
    def rec(node, addr):
        kids = sorted(rec(c, addr + (i,)) for i, c in enumerate(node))
        return '(' + marks.get(addr, '') + ''.join(kids) + ')'
    return rec(t, ())

# ---------- linear maps ----------
def pushforward(V, u):
    """P for pruning leaf u; rows indexed by V\\{u}, columns by V."""
    Y = [w for w in V if w != u]
    P = sp.zeros(len(Y), len(V))
    for j, w in enumerate(V):
        tgt = w[:-1] if w == u else w
        P[Y.index(tgt), j] = 1
    return Y, P

def retract_matrix(V, Z):
    """P_{V->Z}: longest prefix in Z."""
    M = sp.zeros(len(Z), len(V))
    for j, w in enumerate(V):
        k = len(w)
        while w[:k] not in Z:
            k -= 1
        M[Z.index(w[:k]), j] = 1
    return M

def perm_matrix(V, perm):
    n = len(V)
    S = sp.zeros(n, n)
    for j, w in enumerate(V):
        S[V.index(perm.get(w, w)), j] = 1
    return S

def unknown_matrix(n, offset):
    return sp.Matrix(n, n, lambda i, j: sp.Symbol('m%d' % (offset + i * n + j)))

def solve_space(constraints, syms):
    """Basis (list of dicts sym->Rational) of the solution space of homogeneous linear eqs."""
    if constraints:
        A = sp.Matrix([[sp.diff(c, s) for s in syms] for c in constraints])
        ns = A.nullspace()
    else:

        keep = [i for i in range(n) if i != u]
        for b in r['basis']:
            c = Q(b['c'])
            need([Q(x) for x in b['pre']] == [c if i == u else (-c if i == v else Q(0)) for i in range(n)], 'wrong pre value')
            need([Q(x) for x in b['post']] == [c if y == p else (-c if y == v else Q(0)) for y in keep], 'wrong post value')
            need(b['ok'] is True, 'producer row flag')
        need(any(Q(b['c']) != 0 for b in r['basis']) == e['nonzero_c'], 'Nd witness mismatch')
    for r in cert['O_cases']:
        par, pos = tree_from_addresses(r['vertices'])
        need(ahu(par, {pos[tuple(r['p'])]: 'p'}) == r['key'], 'substituted O tree')
        e = expO[r['key']]
        for f in ('n', 'depth', 'carriers', 'dim', 'factorizes'):
            need(r[f] == e[f], 'O value mismatch: ' + f)
    # scientific gates
    gates = {
        'P_formulas_all_cases': all(e['basis_ok'] for e in expP.values()),
        'P_dim_matches_n2_3n_3': all(e['dim'] == e['dim_predicted'] for e in expP.values()),
        'Nd_nonzero_admissible_exists': all(e['nonzero_c'] for e in expP.values()),
        'K_witnesses': all(e['witness']['id']['c'] == '1/5' and e['witness']['tot']['c'] == '0'
                           and e['witness']['tot']['kappa'] == '1' and e['witness']['dif']['kappa'] == '0'
                           and e['witness']['dif']['c'] == '1/5'
                           and all(w['PN'] and w['SX'] for w in e['witness'].values()) for e in expP.values()),
        'N_drop_SX_witness': all(e['drop_SX_pre_receiver_nonzero_exists'] for e in expP.values()),
        'N_drop_PN_witness': all(e['drop_PN_post_receiver_differs_exists'] for e in expP.values()),
        'T233_cone_total_control_fails_to_separate': all(
            all(v == {'pre': '0', 'post': '0'} for v in e['cone_total_control'].values()) for e in expP.values()),
        'O_dim_depth_plus_1_and_factorizes': all(e['factorizes'] for e in expO.values()),
    }
    need(all(gates.values()), 'scientific gate RED: ' + json.dumps(gates, sort_keys=True))
    return {'status': 'GREEN', 'P_cases': len(expP), 'O_cases': len(expO),
            'P_basis_elements': sum(e['dim'] for e in expP.values()),
            'O_carriers': sum(e['carriers'] for e in expO.values()), 'tree_counts': counts, 'gates': gates}

def main():
    cert_path, producer_path = sys.argv[1], sys.argv[2]
    raw = open(cert_path, 'rb').read()
    cert = json.loads(raw)
    try:
        res = verify(cert, open(producer_path, 'rb').read())
    except Reject as exc:
        sys.stdout.write(json.dumps({'status': 'REJECTED', 'reason': str(exc)}, sort_keys=True) + '\n')
        sys.exit(1)
    res['certificate_sha256'] = hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest()
    sys.stdout.write(json.dumps(res, sort_keys=True, indent=1) + '\n')

if __name__ == '__main__':
    main()
