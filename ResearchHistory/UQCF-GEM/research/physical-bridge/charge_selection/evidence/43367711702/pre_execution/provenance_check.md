It says: "The rule preserves total grade, commutes with positive global rescaling, and is functorial." This is a source-measure map, not an operation on a joint Hilbert space. The file was read as text; its functions were not executed.

2. Finite additivity over a general commutative additive monoid does not by itself provide subtraction, kernels, a Hilbert space, or physical preparation of arbitrary signed coordinates.

3. For `X=(o,p,u,v)`, `Y=(o,p,v)`, the exact native law is `Px=(x_o,x_p+x_u,x_v)`. The preserved native extensive total is `1_Y^T Px=1_X^T x`. More general pushforward-compatible scalar weights obey `c_X=P^T c_Y`, hence `c_u=c_p` and zero source deficit. This does not assert that all address weights are equal or that every possible native functional must be conserved.

4. In the supplied normalized diagonal encoding `Pi_X(z)=sum_i z_i |i><i|`, the total functional is `sum_i z_i=Tr(I_S Pi_X(z))=1`. Its canonical diagonal observable is `I_S`, **not** `pi_u`. This identifies the represented total, without claiming that diagonal source encoding fixes arbitrary off-diagonal additions to an observable.

## Narrow previous-candidate comparison and stopping recommendation

`joint_balance_delivery/ATTEMPT.md` §4 already takes `Q_S=sum_j q_j pi_j`, `d=q_u-q_p`, and obtains `C*(Q_S)=Q_S-d pi_u`. It proposes the all-input blank-isometry balance

`W†(Q_S tensor I_ER + d J_R)W=Q_S`, with `J_R=I_SE tensor pi_1,R`.

For `d!=0`, §5 proves this is equivalent to `W†J_R W=pi_u`, and (because both operators are projections) to `J_R W=W pi_u`. At `Q_S=pi_u`, `d=1`, this is exactly the receiver relation sought by the new charge candidate. The earlier text explicitly says its hypothesis is "not, by itself, a claim about a global unitary commuting with a physical total charge."

The old §7 additionally permits a charged E, showing that `Q_E=d pi_1,E` makes W_E and W_R both satisfy total balance. It identifies absent or controlled E contribution as a substantive unsourced premise. The new no-unit-gap H_E condition is a concrete proposed way to control that competing channel; it is not explicitly formulated or derived in these inspected documents.

`REVIEW_RESPONSE.md` preserves the nonzero native restriction deficit but states that it takes the same value under W_R and W_E and does not assign it to a physical receiver account. Its final disposition leaves the native bridge NOT_DERIVED and disallows another weighting of the same source functional from being represented as a repaired native derivation.

Recommendation: **do not stop solely as an exact duplicate** if the authorized task is a bounded study of genuinely new, explicit physical assumptions and the mathematical checker verifies that global conservation plus spectral exclusion yields the needed restriction.
