Conventions for code: a prefix tree P on n nodes is a parent array with parent[0] = none and parent[i] < i (all (n-1)! labelled recursive trees; 34 trees for n <= 5, 10 for n <= 4). An append under l adds node e = n with parent l (and, for two locations l != l', e = n under l and e' = n + 1 under l'). A retained view of P is an ancestor-closed subset containing 0; its lift X' adds e exactly when l is in X (and e' exactly when l' is). A history is a strictly decreasing chain X_1 ⊋ X_2 ⊋ ... of retained views of P, with X_1 allowed to equal P. h_k is the nearest retained ancestor of e in X_k' (e itself if retained). The prefix history's grade is I x_P (x_e = 0 on the lift); the appended history's grade is I x_P + w e_e. Retained coordinates are listed in increasing node order. ## 3. Preregistered exhaustive universes (producer AND checker; counts and identity digests must agree exactly; violations must be 0)

- **U12 (sections 11 and 12, one location):** every one of the 34 trees with n <= 5, every append location l in P, w in {1, 2}, every history of length 1 to 3, every base grade x_P in {0,1}^n. Identity digest: sha256 of the sorted lines "parent|l|chain|holders" (parent with "-" for none; chain = views separated by ";", each view comma-separated; holders comma-separated), one line per (tree, l, history).
- **U114 (Theorem 11.4, two locations):** every tree with n <= 5, every ordered pair l != l', w = 1, every history of length 1 to 3, x_P = 0 and x_P = all ones. - **U13 (section 13, exact laws):** every one of the 10 trees with n <= 4, every l, w in {1, 2}, every history of length 1 or 2, every assignment of an L3 law to each node of P (independent). At every step, with both joint retained laws computed exactly from the full product law by the pushforward: (a) at every retained configuration y, L1(y) nu_h(y_h) = L0(y) (nu_h * delta_w)(y_h) (likelihood factorization), with nu_h computed independently as the convolution of the laws in h's fiber; (b) joint TV = TV(nu_h, nu_h * delta_w) >= max nu_h; (c) for every nonempty proper subset S of the retained addresses, the S-marginal TV is 0 if h is not in S and equals the joint TV if h is in S; (d) TV = 1 when nu_h is a point mass; (e) the success probability of the holder-only test 1{(nu_h * delta_w)(y_h) > nu_h(y_h)} equals (1 + joint TV)/2, the optimum.
