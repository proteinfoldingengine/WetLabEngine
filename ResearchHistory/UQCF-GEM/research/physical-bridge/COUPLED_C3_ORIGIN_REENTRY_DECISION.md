## The actual frontier
The recent admission and record-transfer results are useful conditional mathematics. They do not establish native acquisition of their inputs. The current proved chain begins with a correctly initialized and accessible global record:

global singleton/pair miss field -> outside-root singleton profile -> exact admission bit -> restricted safe repair policy.

The first arrow is the record-transfer identity; the next is the empty-intersection criterion. Their composition is valid WHEN the global field and template are supplied. Global moments are one sufficient route, not a necessary representation of every possible solution.

The next research objective therefore remains acquisition/retention, not further algebraic simplification of the decoder or another conditional repair protocol.
