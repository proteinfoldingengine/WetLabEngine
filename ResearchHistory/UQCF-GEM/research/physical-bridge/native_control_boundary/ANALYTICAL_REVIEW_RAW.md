### Reachability and readout

For any finite path S_1 to E, prepend the finite S_0-to-S_1 path. This proves Reach_G1(S_1) is contained in Reach_G1(S_0); exchange indices for equality. E need not be exact. The conclusion applies to every state in the shared connected component, without asserting that every band-admitted state is connected to an exact preparation.

This is historical source certification. It is not a claim that current states can never be distinguished or that no conditional correlation can occur under a separately specified interaction.
