**Conventions.** Trees are parent arrays with parent[0] = none and parent[i] < i; Genesis o = 0. Carriers are prefix-closed subsets containing 0. "max nu" = the largest atom of nu. Fractions are written in lowest terms "a/b", integers "a"; a law is a list of [value, probability] string pairs sorted by value (numeric).
