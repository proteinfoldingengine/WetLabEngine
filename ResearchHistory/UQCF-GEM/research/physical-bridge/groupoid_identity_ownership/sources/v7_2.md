# UQCF-GEM v7.2 — Fully Recoverable Groupoid Core and Positivity

## Theorem

Let \(\mathcal G\) be the total uniquely recoverable sector of retained histories. If every morphism in this sector has a genuine inverse, \(\mathcal G\) is a groupoid.

Linearize it over \(\mathbb C\). For

\[
a=\sum_{g\in\mathcal G} c_g g,
\qquad
a^\dagger=\sum_g \overline{c_g}\,g^{-1},
\]

define, for each object \(u\),

\[
\omega_u(a)=\text{coefficient of }1_u.
\]

Then

\[
\omega_u(a^\dagger a)
=
\sum_{g:s(g)=u}|c_g|^2\ge 0.
\]

Hence \(\omega_u\) is a normalized positive linear functional.

This is important because positivity is obtained from the retained recoverable algebraic structure; a Hilbert space does not have to be postulated first. GNS can be applied afterward.

## Boundary

This directly closes A4 only on the fully recoverable/groupoid core. The full pruning structure can be irreversible and need not be a groupoid. The next unresolved step is whether native pruning itself becomes a conditional expectation onto a retained subalgebra.
