## 2. First distinction: which composition?

There are at least three mathematically different operations:

1. Sequential composition of transformations or composable provenance morphisms.
2. Parallel combination of independent source-bearing systems.
3. Union/intersection of compatibility constraints or gluing specifications.

They need not induce the same additive law. A reversibility obstruction for operation composition does not rule out a positive charge additive under tensor product. An idempotence obstruction for constraint conjunction does not show all richer gluing composition is idempotent.

Before applying any theorem below, identify the actual operation, its unit, its domain, and the equivalences imposed on it. Atemporal composition does not require physical chronology.

## 3. Exact descent criterion for multiplicity

Suppose raw provenance words form a monoid P and legitimate presentation equivalence ~ is a composition congruence. Let d:P -> R satisfy d(pq)=d(p)+d(q), d(e)=0.

**Proposition.** There is a well-defined additive D:P/~ -> R with d=D composed with the quotient map if and only if d(p)=d(q) whenever p~q.

Proof: Necessity follows because a function has one value on each equivalence class. Conversely define D([p])=d(p); fiber constancy makes it well-defined, and additivity descends. The same argument applies to composable arrows in a category with matching interfaces.

For a monoid presentation with generators a and relations u~v, assign generator weights w_a. The additive word value is the sum of generator weights with multiplicity. It descends precisely when every defining relation has equal total weight on its two sides. Stability under composition then makes all generated relations preserve that value.

An unweighted event count therefore fails whenever one legitimate event can be subdivided into two equivalent events. Weighted additivity can survive subdivision only if the primitive weights are independently supplied and satisfy the relation; invariance does not select those weights by itself.

### Universal additive object

Define the abelian group U(P) with symbols [p] and relations [pq]=[p]+[q], [e]=0, and [p]=[q] for legitimate equivalences. Every real additive invariant factors uniquely through a group homomorphism U(P)->R.

This is a classification device, not a physical source construction. The allowed nonnegative invariants are the homomorphisms whose values on all provenance classes are nonnegative. A vector-space dimension or a preferred positive ray can only be discussed after these objects and constraints are specified. Even one positive ray leaves normalization unselected unless another premise fixes it.

## 4. Two immediate algebraic obstructions

### Reversible-composition obstruction

Suppose each provenance arrow p has an inverse p^-1 modulo legitimate equivalence, and D takes values in the nonnegative reals. Then

0 = D(e) = D(p^-1 p) = D(p^-1)+D(p).

Both summands are nonnegative, so both are zero. Thus a nonnegative additive invariant vanishes on the reversible sector.

A slightly stronger conditional version needs only a generalized inverse q satisfying p q p ~ p: additivity gives 2D(p)+D(q)=D(p), hence D(p)+D(q)=0; nonnegativity again forces D(p)=D(q)=0.

Signed real invariants are not ruled out on arbitrary groups. Finite-order elements nevertheless have zero real additive value because p^n~e implies nD(p)=0. Do not generalize this finite-order argument to an arbitrary infinite history monoid. No claim is made here that native provenance identifies a reversible loop with the identity merely because its quantum endpoint returns.

### Idempotence obstruction

If p p ~ p, additivity gives 2D(p)=D(p), hence D(p)=0. This holds even for signed real values. Therefore repeat-insensitive constraint conjunction cannot faithfully encode repeat-sensitive real additive strength through the conjunction result alone.

If a source-to-constraint map sends repeated sources to the same constraints, a nonzero source count may still exist upstream. It simply cannot be recovered from that idempotent image. Additional retained multiplicity is then real extra structure, whose action must separately be justified.

These obstructions do not prove that every pre-pruning source is zero. They identify combinations of assumptions that cannot support the proposed positive additive defect.

## 5. Correction to the approved extendability test

The earlier proposed test compared p and q with equal present visible data, different multiplicity, and different allowed extensions. That is insufficient on its own to establish a new source-to-kinematics coupling.

Let B be the frozen quantum configurations, O:B->Y the observation map, and C a fixed space of extension candidates with matching declared interfaces. Let R subset B x C be the frozen quantum compatibility relation. Define

Ext_0(x) = {c : R(x,c)}.

For visible data y, the existential extension set is

Ext_0(y) = union over x with O(x)=y of Ext_0(x).

Two different hidden configurations x and x' can have the same O-value but different Ext_0(x) and Ext_0(x'). That would identify hidden-completion information under the same law R. It does not show R changed.

Consequently, finding such a pair could be useful as a completion witness, but would not meet the stronger mechanism claim in this branch. This distinction is a correction to the proposal, not a newly discovered effect in a native ensemble.

If p and q are equivalent under *all* admissibility-sensitive extension contexts, different extension sets contradict that equivalence by definition. We must distinguish this full contextual equivalence from present-data equality or presentation-only equivalence. The same word “equivalent” cannot serve all three roles.

## 6. Matched conditional extension criterion

Suppose a separately justified relation B_p subset B specifies which quantum configurations realize provenance sector p. Define

Ext_p(y) = {c : there exists x in B_p with O(x)=y and R(x,c)}.

An extension candidate c is absent from Ext_p(y) if and only if every baseline completion x with O(x)=y and R(x,c) lies outside B_p. This is the existential-to-universal projection criterion of v9.43 applied to extensions.

To attribute a conditional extension difference to provenance, the relation B_p must be supplied independently of the desired result. To attribute it specifically to the multiplicity D rather than other provenance information, show that the declared extension prediction factors through D (and the retained interface/data): equal D and matched retained data must give the same prediction on the stated domain.

Even then the result is a conditional compatibility law. Whether the extended provenance theory makes B_p a fundamental kinematic sector or merely a selected subset must be stated explicitly. A difference in conditioned extension sets does not overturn the fixed-image theorem.

## 7. Why additivity alone cannot supply the missing coupling

Take any baseline quantum configuration space B and any provenance monoid P with an additive invariant D. Form the decorated space B x P. Permit extensions solely by the baseline relation R on B, independent of P. All presentation invariance and additivity properties of D remain true; quantum extension possibilities do not depend on it.

This is a logical countermodel, not an adopted UQCF extension. It proves that additivity and well-defined multiplicity alone cannot force a compatibility response. If P and D also have an appropriate covariance action, the product construction inherits it while the quantum extension relation can remain provenance-blind. Extra reduction or gluing axioms would need to be specified and checked separately; they are not presumed satisfied by an unspecified model.

The neutral P sector reproduces the baseline quantum sector. Thus neutral reduction and composable multiplicity by themselves do not remove the decoupled possibility.
