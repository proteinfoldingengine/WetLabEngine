## 5. Exact missing interface

Relative to the milestone and the sharpened obligation, the inspected packet is missing all of the following (any one may re-enter; inventing one is forbidden):

1. **Source-independent algebra refinement law.** A derived rule producing A_{k+1} (or a justified admissible class of such refinements) from already native premises, with Z(A_k) ⊆ A_{k+1}, not parameterized by the source label b, and not merely “choose a useful RAS.”
2. **Forced center growth.** A proof that some justified native event/state class requires Z(A_k) ⊊ Z(A_{k+1}) (strict), rather than permitting non-growing centers (C1/C5).
3. **Justified central-reader association.** A derivation that a retained receiver's accessible effects are (or include) Z(A_m)'s central-atom POVM — factorization, reference binding and retention — rather than interpretive superselection reading (P2).
4. **Optional but eligible:** a probabilistic consequence forced uniformly across a justified class that already includes (1)–(3); or a precise impossibility theorem for a *bounded* declared candidate class of purported growth laws (not a vague global negative).

Until one of these enters with immutable provenance, the center-access-growth theorem remains conditional on supplied P1a and P2, and the native milestone remains open.
