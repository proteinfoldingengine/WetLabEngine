## Scientific objective

The next important advance must constrain the missing native relation

native event/source update → receiver-owned retained output.

Do not spend the next research cycle producing another conditional consumer, decoder, response menu, holding map, profile statistic, or alternate P1 representation unless it directly removes part of this missing relation.

The project already has substantial mathematics showing that, once an event is legitimately typed as a native P1 pruning of an extensive grade:

1. the retained ancestral retraction is fixed;
2. the extensive-grade pushforward is fixed by finite additivity;
3. a single source difference is transferred to the corresponding retained owner coordinate;
4. the canonical retained address factor containing that coordinate is mathematically identifiable;
5. central classical effects can distinguish the resulting retained states nondisturbingly.

These should now be treated as established mathematical infrastructure within their accepted scope, not repeatedly re-derived.

The current unresolved scientific question is narrower:

What inherited native structure forces the actual joint event to deliver the source distinction to the identified receiver-owned output rather than to some other complementary subsystem?

The accepted P1 receiver-location counterexample is the key boundary. Two joint realizations can implement the same source-side P1 channel while placing the distinction in different complementary outputs. Therefore source correspondence alone does not determine receiver delivery.

New work must confront that fact directly.

## Required work sequence

### 1. Freeze the exact missing implication

Start from the strongest currently accepted premises and write the target implication explicitly.

For example:

Given:

* an actual occurring event;
* a native same-Genesis pruning X→Y;
* the inherited P1 extensive-grade update;
* an initially blind designated receiver R;
* the mathematically identified P1 owner r(u);

derive, rather than assume, one of the following:

A. R is the output subsystem corresponding to r(u); or
B. a specific receiver-owned observable/effect of R must change; or
C. every joint realization satisfying an inherited native constraint gives the same nonzero receiver consequence.

Do not weaken the target into “some complementary system contains the information.” That is already known and is insufficient.

### 2. Search only inherited structures that could constrain joint realization

Prioritize existing native objects that may tie the event to a receiver-owned output:

* lineage and ancestry ownership;
* retained-source identity;
* receiver/owner correspondence already present in P1;
* retained algebra or record compatibility;
* native subsystem ownership;
* existing actual-record primitives;
* provenance binding;
* preservation/retention constraints;
* composition or compatibility relations that act on both source and receiver;
* any inherited rule that distinguishes the P1 owner from an arbitrary environment factor.

The question is not whether these structures are mathematically convenient.

The question is:

Do they eliminate the blind realization while retaining the informative realization?

If a proposed premise does not distinguish those two realizations, it does not advance the frontier.

### 3. Use the blind/informative pair as a standing adversarial test

The existing P1 receiver-location result should be treated as the canonical challenge.

For every proposed native constraint K, test:

* Does W_R satisfy K?
* Does W_E satisfy K?
* If both satisfy K, K does not determine receiver delivery.
* If only W_R satisfies K, prove why K is inherited rather than inserted.
* If neither satisfies K, determine whether the candidate is simply unrelated to the actual native model.

This test should happen before launching a large numerical or review campaign.

### 4. Prefer an invariant or necessity theorem over a designed mechanism

The strongest result would look like:

For every joint realization J compatible with native premises N,

receiver_contrast(J) ≠ 0.

or

the receiver-owned output factor is uniquely determined by N.

or

the complementary location freedom is removed by an inherited ownership/retention condition.

A weaker but still important result is acceptable if it proves that a clearly identified inherited condition eliminates a substantial class of blind realizations.

Do not choose a coupling because it works and then attempt to justify it afterward.

### 5. Keep physical and mathematical claims separate

A theorem may establish a necessary mathematical consequence without yet proving a laboratory realization.

Report separately:

* native premises;
* newly derived consequence;
* what remains supplied;
* whether the result constrains receiver delivery;
* whether it produces an accessible observable distinction;
* whether the result has physical interpretation or remains mathematical.

Do not promote canonical factorization, centrality, source pushforward, or a software consumer into physical access by terminology alone.

## Research admission gate

Before starting a new substantial work item, answer all five:

1. What exact missing receiver-delivery premise does this candidate attack?
2. What inherited primitive could remove that premise?
3. Which existing blind realization is the candidate intended to exclude?
4. What measurable or receiver-owned consequence would follow if successful?
5. What result would falsify the candidate?

If these cannot be answered precisely, deprioritize the work.

## High-value possible targets

The following are worth pursuing:

### Target A — native ownership theorem

Determine whether the inherited meaning of the P1 owner r(u) already carries a stronger ownership property than “coordinate receiving the pushforward.”

Can ownership be derived as an operational state factor or retained subsystem relation that excludes routing the complementary information elsewhere?

### Target B — joint-event compatibility

Search for an inherited compatibility/naturality condition on the full event, not just its reduced source channel, that constrains complementary outputs.

The condition must be independently motivated by existing UQCF-GEM structure.

### Target C — retained-record necessity

Determine whether an actual retained-record primitive forces the information associated with a removed source to remain in the P1 owner’s retained record.

Do not assume the record. Derive its source association and ownership.

### Target D — realization-invariant observable

Search for a receiver observable whose contrast is fixed by the native premises across every allowed joint realization.

This would avoid needing a unique microscopic coupling.

### Target E — sharpened impossibility boundary

If none of the inherited structures can distinguish informative and blind realizations, prove the smallest additional premise required.

The goal would then be to state exactly what physical postulate is missing, rather than continuing to rediscover variants of the same gap.

## Work that should now be deprioritized

Unless directly needed for one of the targets above, do not spend major effort on:

* another P1 propagation theorem;
* another response graph;
* another legality decoder;
* another fixed-profile consumer;
* another supplied controller;
* another holding map;
* another conditional copy/register construction;
* another source-only calibration;
* another numerical campaign demonstrating a supplied mechanism works;
* another theorem whose conclusion is only that “some information survives somewhere.”

Those may be mathematically valid, but they do not address the current frontier.

## Verification discipline

For a material new result:

1. freeze the exact theorem and source dependencies;
2. record all assumptions with explicit tags such as INHERITED, DERIVED, SUPPLIED, or NEW ASSUMPTION;
3. attempt the W_R / W_E adversarial comparison first;
4. preserve counterexamples and failed versions;
5. if bounded computation is needed, freeze the domain and controls before execution;
6. independently reconstruct the mathematical claim;
7. perform publication/source readback;
8. do not infer physical closure from a successful workflow.

## Success criterion

A scientifically important advance occurs if the work establishes one of the following:

* an inherited condition that forces the P1 distinction into the receiver-owned output;
* a realization-independent nonzero receiver observable consequence;
* a proof that the receiver factor is uniquely fixed by native structure;
* or an exact theorem identifying the minimal genuinely new physical premise required for receiver delivery.

Anything weaker should be reported as supporting mathematics, not as closure of the native interaction bridge.

The objective is no longer to show that UQCF-GEM can contain conditional source-to-receiver constructions.

The objective is to determine why the native event must deliver its distinction to this receiver.
