## Outcome
- Preregistered conjecture C FAILED at n=6. C said only a co-merged survivor/removed pair with isomorphic past fiber chains breaks invariance (with the singleton, single-removal exception).
  There were 40 mismatches, all in one direction: C held but 𝒮 was not invariant. C is necessary for invariance but not sufficient. In all 34k cases, C-bad implied non-invariant.
  Counterexample: parents (·,0,0,0,1,4). Prune {2,5}, then {3,4}. The survivors 0 and 1 end with fiber chains {0,2,3} ⊃ {0,2} and {1,4,5} ⊃ {4,5},
  which are isomorphic as leveled dendrograms. In the first the level-1 pair holds the owner; in the second it is the removed atom. The chain swap
  {0,2,3}↔{1,4,5} preserves every π_j but maps shells {0},{2},{3} | {1},{4,5} to a different partition. So the label is needed even with no co-merged isomorphism.
- POSTHOC revised criterion C4, labelled and disclosed in PREREG.md: 𝒮 is G-invariant iff C holds AND every pair of sibling blocks with isomorphic plain leveled dendrograms has the same owner-decorated
  canonical form (at each node, mark the child that contains the owner). Sibling pairs are taken at every node, including the virtual root over π_m.
  C4 matched invariance on all cases n ≤ 6: 0 mismatches, with 19,262 invariant out of 32,478 at n=6, counted over labelled parent arrays, not unlabelled trees.
  The "only if" direction is proved: each violation gives an explicit sibling-swap automorphism that moves 𝒮. The "if" direction is NOT proved. It is supported only by the brute-force count,
  and a count isn't a proof.
