## 3. Finite-prefix actions are trivial

For any homomorphism alpha:G->S, where S is a finite permutation group, put N=|S|. Every g in G has an Nth root h in G: diagonalize each unitary block and divide each eigenphase by N. Hence alpha(g)=alpha(h)^N=identity, by the finite-group order theorem. Thus alpha is trivial without a continuity assumption.

Apply this to every alpha_n in (3). Each cylinder pi_n^(-1)(C) is G-invariant. Covariance then gives

    E(pi_n^(-1)(C)) = g E(pi_n^(-1)(C)) g*   for every g in G,

so the effect lies in Z(A). It follows from (1) that p_0 and p_1 agree on every complete finite-prefix event. This step is the inherited finite-register result, applied with its actual hypotheses; it is not counted as a new interaction.

Equivalently, a G action by rooted-tree automorphisms on a locally finite rooted tree is trivial at every finite level and therefore fixes every infinite path. A label permutation symmetry of the tree is not thereby identified with G; (3) still needs independent justification.

    delta_TV(p_0,p_1) = sup_n delta_TV(p_0|F_n,p_1|F_n).          (5)
