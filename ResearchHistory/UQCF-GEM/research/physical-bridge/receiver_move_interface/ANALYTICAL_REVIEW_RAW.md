The one-command conditions are necessary and sufficient:

- For addition, the incidence must be absent and the parent incidence present. Existing nesting already excludes the added index from all children when it is absent from the vertex. An addition cannot empty a support.
- For deletion, the incidence must be present and the vertex must be a tip of that index's view. Otherwise a child retains the index after its parent loses it, violating nesting.
