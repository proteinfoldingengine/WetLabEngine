2. **Positive-operator inequality (equation 2).** The proof is valid for arbitrary positive X, including rank-deficient and unnormalized X. For a rank-one operator, Schmidt rank k is at most q, and Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the squared sum of the k amplitudes by k times their squared-amplitude sum. The induced measurement is available for each full-output measurement in the declared optimization. Maximizing proves p_guess(RQ)<=q^2 p_guess(R) for arbitrary priors and arbitrary output states.
