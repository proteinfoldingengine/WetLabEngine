## Scientific argument and source semantics

1. **The milestone has the correct scope.** The report permits a derived probabilistic output law, allows nonunique microscopic mechanisms when a common accessible consequence is forced, and does not require deterministic outcomes, guaranteed progress, gravity, or completion of the C3 marker protocol. Requiring justified record access is material to the requested accessible consequence. The initial-answer exclusion is correctly qualified as an acquisition condition. The report does not quantify over every imaginable dynamics.

2. **V930-to-V906 is a directly observed source dependency.** V930's `V906_PATH` names the recovered implementation filename; `load_v906` loads that module; `generate_fresh` invokes `v906.run_case(seed, n, geom, mode)`. `run` separately reads the V928 frozen rule table and applies it. The report is correct to distinguish this implementation dependency from the later V957/V960 documentary references. This establishes the source-level linkage, not a cryptographically pinned record of what bytes an old runtime actually loaded.

3. **The update and its carrier are supplied in the recovered construction.** V906 fixes `CSTAR`, `THRESH`, periodic finite-difference geometry, the initial fields, defect construction, and the mode list. `run_case` branches on that chosen mode. `directed_variational` uses normalized negative `semi_derivative` and backtracking with `e2 <= e0 + 1e-12`; other branches explicitly prescribe forcing, target relaxation, noise, or holding. The draft accurately calls this a diagnostic descent check without claiming an exact native physical energy law. The missing derivation conclusion rests on these supplied ingredients and access semantics; it does not follow merely from V906's disclaimer about lack of a *unique* repair law.

4. **The source-role input is explicitly mode-family information.** V906's `source_family` maps named active/passive/structured modes into their corresponding family and otherwise returns `rejected_or_broken_source`. V930 populates `source_family` from `mode`, then maps it through `SOURCE_ROLE_MAP`: active to active role, passive and structured to the same eligible-nonactive role, and rejected/broken to rejected/broken role. V930's quotient also consults `source_family` for the structured failed-basin branch, in addition to start/end basin status, accepted steps and reduction. The draft's narrow claim is therefore supported: these Q/S labels are constructed from the declared diagnostic and family inputs, not newly justified receiver records. This does not prohibit physical system-side dependence on its state.

5. **V906's self-agreement is real and correctly localized.** In `main`, both `true_class` and `pred_class` are assigned by `ep.apply(seven_class, axis=1)` on the same rows. Agreement between these columns is not independent taxonomy validation. Other V906 comparisons and constructed trajectories are not thereby erased. The report does not accuse V970 of using this definition twice, and correctly notes that V930 has a separate, unrecovered V928 table. That distinction matters.

6. **The V970/V971 result is correctly described as predictive validation.** V970 explicitly asks whether emitted Q/S symbols are predictable from event dynamics; V971's specification says this uses certified-domain models. The selected CSV and JSON records agree on 240/240 Q/S-pair matches for each V957/V960 control, 37/420 for the near-manifold mimic, and leave-domain-out pair accuracy 0.8764880952380952. The mimic passes the archived behavior and radius checks while failing the added criterion. The draft preserves this bounded positive evidence. Predictive agreement alone does not identify a native transition law, establish causal generation in the stronger sense sought by the user, or earn receiver access. The report does not infer the missing model, training design, split independence, or leakage from these scores, and does not reject stochastic laws for imperfect prediction.

7. **Later ancestry is not overstated.** The V957 report describes fresh transfer beyond V930; V960 describes a harder independent trajectory family. The complete recovered file-member inventories contain trajectories, ledgers, results, reports and figures, but no generation or fitting implementation source. The actual V957/V960 trajectory headers include `mode`, `driver_mode`, and `source_family`; their ledger headers include aggregate diagnostics and Q/S labels. Those schemas support the report's diagnostic-input observation, not a claim that the later stages reused exact V906 bytes. The draft expressly preserves this distinction and limits the absence finding to the inspected archives.

8. **The route finding is an evidence assessment, not a universal theorem.** The inspected sources establish supplied construction plus bounded compatibility checks and do not supply the requested native derivation. That warrants the OPEN milestone disposition. It does not prove that no native law exists, invalidate the archived mimic controls, reopen the stopped quantum-source selector branch, or overturn the inherited conditional RAS/RCR, retained-source, inclusion, and response results. The report retains these boundaries and does not smuggle in physical force, energy, metric, gravity, or fundamental-time claims.
