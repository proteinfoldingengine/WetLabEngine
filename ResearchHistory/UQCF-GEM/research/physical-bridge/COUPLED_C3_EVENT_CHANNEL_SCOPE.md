## Goal

Test whether the already-defined CORE retained observables, even supplemented with native-event occurrence counts and protected-band/floor status, can determine the spectator capacity bit b=1[Z nonempty] without the spectator-toggle directions. Do not assume a complete signed event channel.

## Frozen model

Finite spectator palette T with at least two distinct labels u,v, and fresh w outside T. Prepared roots:
r1={a,b}, r2={b,c}, r3={a,c}, r4={d,e} union Z.
Original floors all 2. Native spectator events add an absent or delete a present spectator on r4. Every prepared state has tau=3 and all floors hold.

Observer channel O_coarse at every retained slice:
- labelled core projections on {a,b,c,d,e} of every root;
- all core-only residual miss counts M_core(H) for nonempty H subseteq core of size<=4;
- exact global hitting number tau and Boolean floor-validity vector;

- known initial empty spectator seed Z0=empty.

## Required theorem

1. Exhibit two valid histories of the same length, with the SAME O_coarse transcript at every slice, but different final b. Suggested pair: +u,-u versus +u,+v. Check all intermediate roots, tau and floor2.
2. Conclude no deterministic function of O_coarse transcript computes final b for both worlds, despite known seed and complete event occurrence/root address.
3. Show what additional distinction suffices for THIS pair: direction of the second event. Distinguish this local separating bit from a general proof that all signed event channels are natively available.
4. Show the full-palette miss-count singleton coordinates M_R({z}) would distinguish the final states IF initialized, but core-only M_core does not.
5. State that an observer supplied exact r4 cardinality, floor slack, or the full spectator event directions is outside O_coarse and can resolve the ambiguity.
6. Do not infer a universal no-observer theorem, physical forces, geometry, energy, GR/ADM or fundamental time.
