# UQCF-GEM v9.33 Atemporal Reduction-Order Theorem

## 1. Reductions, orders, and successor laws

Let \(\mathcal A=M_d(\mathbb C)\). A trace-preserving conditional expectation onto a unital \(*\)-subalgebra \(\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A\) is a completely positive, unital, idempotent map

\[
E_{\mathcal B}:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B
\]

that fixes \(\mathcal B\). If

\[
\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal A,
\]

then the trace-orthogonal expectations satisfy

\[
E_{\mathcal B}E_{\mathcal C}=E_{\mathcal B}.
\]

Subalgebra inclusion therefore defines a genuine information-reduction partial order.

However, the partial order does not select an edge, chain, or successor. It answers which reductions are coarser after a family has been supplied; it does not state which reduction happens. Likewise, a data-processing inequality states that a quantity is monotone under a chosen channel. It does not choose the channel.

## 2. Unitary-naturality obstruction

Suppose no structure beyond a finite-dimensional Hilbert space is supplied, and a rule assigns a unital \(*\)-subalgebra \(\mathcal B\subseteq M_d\) naturally under every unitary:

\[
U\mathcal B U^\dagger=\mathcal B
\qquad\forall U\in U(d).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\mathcal B=\mathbb CI\quad\text{or}\quad\mathcal B=M_d.}
\]

Indeed, if \(\mathcal B\) contains a nonscalar Hermitian operator, invariance supplies its full unitary orbit. The real span of the traceless part of that orbit is the entire traceless Hermitian space, so \(\mathcal B=M_d\). Without additional structure, only the identity and complete-depolarization extremes are natural.

## 3. Tensor-factor erasure poset

For

\[
\mathcal H=\bigotimes_{i=1}^{N}\mathcal H_i
\]

and subset \(S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}\), define

\[
\mathcal B_S=M(\mathcal H_S)\otimes I_{\bar S}
\]

and

\[
E_S(A)=\frac{1}{d_{\bar S}}\operatorname{Tr}_{\bar S}(A)\otimes I_{\bar S}.
\]

Then

\[
S\subseteq T\Longrightarrow E_SE_T=E_S.
\]

The resulting Boolean lattice is canonical once the tensor factors are given. But it has \(N!\) maximal chains. If the factors are otherwise equivalent, the permutation group acts transitively on subsets of a fixed cardinality; no proper nonempty subset is invariant under all permutations. Tensor factorization supplies possible erasures, not a distinguished first erasure or path.

## 4. Locality filtration obstruction

The linear span of weight-at-most-\(k\) observables is generally not a \(*\)-subalgebra. For example, two one-body observables on different factors multiply to a two-body observable. Therefore there is generally no conditional expectation whose range is exactly the locality-\(k\) linear span.

Worse, the unital algebra generated by all one-body matrix algebras is already

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Alg}^*\left(\bigcup_iM(\mathcal H_i)\right)
=M\left(\bigotimes_i\mathcal H_i\right).
}
\]

Thus replacing the linear span by its generated algebra collapses the proposed filtration at its first nontrivial stage.

## 5. Generator-spectral expectation

For a Hermitian native generator with spectral projectors \(P_\alpha\), the pinching map

\[
E_K(A)=\sum_\alpha P_\alpha A P_\alpha

\]

is canonical, basis covariant, CPTP in the Schrödinger picture, unital in the Heisenberg picture, and idempotent. It is a legitimate one-shot information reduction.

But every generator eigenstate is fixed:

\[
E_K(|E_\alpha\rangle\langle E_\alpha|)
=|E_\alpha\rangle\langle E_\alpha|.
\]

The native states used in the frozen ensemble are generator eigenstates, so this map produces no native pruning event.

Coarser spectral partitions form another lattice, not a selected path. A staged flag requires a partition index, grouping rule, or spectral threshold. Sorting and grouping by eigenvalues returns to spectral magnitude and spectral-edge structure, already controlled and excluded as a gravity discriminator at v9.17.

## 6. Compatibility structures

The already-certified compatibility results do not supply the missing selector:

- v9.08: convex exterior admissibility is source independent after source elimination;
- v9.29: scalar source conditioning filters pure completions only by purity and rank;
- v9.30: purification-gauge invariants descend completely to the exterior completion;
- v9.31–v9.32: the native generator supplies neither a stable source-sector commutant nor an independent exact low-body charge.

These objects constrain what is possible, but none selects which information must be discarded next.

## 7. Main adjudication

The frozen structure supplies several kinematic reduction posets and one canonical spectral pinching. It supplies no nontrivial, nonspectral, state-changing successor law.

\[
\boxed{
\texttt{KINEMATIC\_REDUCTION\_POSETS\_EXIST}
\atop
\texttt{NO\_INTRINSIC\_IRREVERSIBLE\_SUCCESSOR\_RULE\_DERIVED}
}
\]

This is not a proof that emergent time is impossible. It identifies a missing mathematical ingredient: a law selecting a proper information reduction from the available poset. Adding such a selector would be a new postulate or a newly derived consequence of additional structure, not a reinterpretation of the current static model.
