## Exact source-reduction theorem

Let

\[
|\Psi\rangle=|0\rangle|a\rangle+|1\rangle|b\rangle,
\]

with

\[
p_0=\lVert a\rVert^2,\qquad
p_1=\lVert b\rVert^2,\qquad
q=\langle Z_s\rangle=p_0-p_1.
\]

The nonselective source Lüders map is

\[
\Delta_{Z_s}(\rho)
=\sum_{z=0,1}(|z\rangle\langle z|\otimes I)\rho(|z\rangle\langle z|\otimes I).
\]

It has three exact properties.

### Exterior invisibility

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_s\Delta_{Z_s}(|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|)
=\operatorname{Tr}_s|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|.
\]

Every exterior observable is unchanged. The discarded information is entirely in the global coherence between the two source sectors.

### Global information loss

\[
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\Delta_{Z_s}(|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|)^2\right]
=p_0^2+p_1^2
=\frac{1+q^2}{2}.
\]

For \(|q|<1\), the pure global state becomes mixed.

### Exact state-change magnitude

\[
\left\lVert
|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|-\Delta_{Z_s}(|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|)
\right\rVert_F^2
=\frac{1-q^2}{2}.
\]

The selective branches have probabilities

\[
p_\pm=\frac{1\pm q}{2}.
\]

This creates an exact bridge between the scalar source coordinate studied in v9.29 and a possible irreversible loss of globally hidden coherence.

## Remaining selector obstructions

### Covariance alone gives only extremes

A structure-free channel covariant under every unitary conjugation has depolarizing form

\[
\Phi_a(A)=aA+(1-a)\frac{\operatorname{Tr}A}{d}I.
\]

Idempotence requires \(a=0\) or \(a=1\): complete depolarization or identity. Proper intermediate pruning requires distinguished structure such as \(Q_s\).

### Symmetry forbids a deterministic first choice

For equivalent tensor factors, permutations act transitively on possible first-factor erasures. No deterministic permutation-equivariant first choice exists. A uniform distribution is invariant, but realizing one branch requires a stochastic law or fixed quantum instrument.

### Input-selected channels are generally nonlinear

The verifier constructed two valid dephasing channels and chose between them using the unknown input state. Every branch was CPTP, but the combined rule violated convex linearity by Frobenius norm \(0.3\). A physical state-dependent branch rule must be supplied as a fixed instrument rather than called a channel after the fact.

### Compatibility alarms do not choose events

A compatibility-failure predicate can say that a state is eligible for reduction. If multiple successors remain symmetry equivalent, the predicate does not choose one. Previously certified convex and pure-completion structures have not supplied the missing symmetry-breaking selector.
