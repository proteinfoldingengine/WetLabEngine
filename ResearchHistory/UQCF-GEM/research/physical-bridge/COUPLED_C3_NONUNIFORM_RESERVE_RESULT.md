## Static criterion for arbitrary positive multiplicities
A safe static reserve exists iff either G has an isolated vertex, or there is an independent set I in G with |I|>|N_G(I)| containing AT MOST ONE type of source multiplicity1. Equivalent local witnesses for no-isolate five-vertex G:
1. Two degree-one types sharing a neighbor (a cherry), with at least one of the two source multiplicities>=2.
2. An independent triple I with neighborhood size2, with at least two of its three multiplicities>=2.

Necessity: expand each active label of a hypothetical safe static reserve to an original maximal container. The resulting type counts n_i are nonnegative integers with sum n_i<=A-1, and all pair floors hold. All pair roots have floor>=1, so at most one n_i is zero. Let d_i=max(a_i-n_i,0), p_i=max(n_i-a_i,0). Since sum d_i>sum p_i, the integral layer-cake identities imply there is h>=1 with |D_h|>|P_h|, where D_h={i:d_i>=h}, P_h={i:p_i>=h}. Each saturated edge from i in D_h forces n_j-a_j>=a_i-n_i>=h. Thus D_h is independent and N(D_h) subset P_h. Any type in D_h with a_i=1 has n_i=0, so at most one such type exists.

If G has no isolates, N(D_h) is nonempty. On five vertices an independent deficient set has size2,3 or4. Size2 has neighborhood size1 and is a cherry. Size3 with neighborhood1 permits any two of its members as a cherry, choosing at least one multiplicity>=2; size3/neighborhood2 is the triple witness. Size4 has neighborhood1 and also yields an eligible cherry. This yields the two stated local alternatives.

Sufficiency: for any eligible deficient independent I, set n_i=a_i-1 on I, n_i=a_i+1 on N(I), and n_i=a_i elsewhere. Saturated floors hold since every neighbor of a decreased type is increased; no saturated edge joins two decreased types. At most one n_i is zero, so all nonedge floors1 hold and at least four maximal types remain occupied. Their maxima admit an actual four-label cover; thus the static core is safe with fewer than A active labels. The omitted labels are retained as empty labels in the same original palette. An isolated vertex alternatively permits deleting one original label in4 toggles, preserving four anchors from other types.
