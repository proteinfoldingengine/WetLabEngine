Let mu be a nonnegative finite measure on X. This is a declared positive-grade mathematical instance; neither a physical probability interpretation nor preparability is derived by normalizing it.

Suppose a proposed record is represented by an event E subset X. Identifying this event indicator with the actual RCR projection is itself still an obligation. If E is a union of complete r-fibers, there is a unique D subset Y with E=r^{-1}(D). Finite additivity then gives the unnormalized identity

    r_*(mu restricted to E) = (r_*mu) restricted to D.      (2)

Proof: at y, the left side sums mu(x) over r(x)=y and x in E. If y is in D this is the whole fiber sum; otherwise it is zero. This is exactly the right side.

Since mu(E)=(r_*mu)(D), for positive mu(E) normalization gives

    r_*(mu(. | E)) = (r_*mu)(. | D).                       (3)

These are commuting mathematical descriptions. They assert no chronological order, event occurrence, outcome sampling or physical interchangeability of two operations.

Equivalently, (2) holds for every nonnegative mu with the same E,D exactly when E=r^{-1}(D), as unit masses test each x.
