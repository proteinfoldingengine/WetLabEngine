1. Finite additive physical grades: whatever additive value structure is actually supplied; no universal real scalar axiom is inferred.

The forward/reverse correspondence and linearity are proved in P0. Integral differences of unit atoms generate formal signed distinctions; rationalization is a mathematical representation, not a derivation of new physically preparable sources.

Every kernel/Green/quotient statement below concerns this inherited linear diagnostic. Extension from Q to R follows from the proofs over characteristic-zero fields and the nonzero rational determinants, not from testing rational examples. Whether all formal directions represent physical retained source operations remains UNRESOLVED_PHYSICAL_REALIZATION. Absolute observer source normalization remains unearned.

Relabelings may rename branch tokens and reorder storage, but do not change ancestry or identify different origins. P,I,R,H are claimed natural in these commuting retained diagrams. They do not contain retained atom labels or a section. P3's no-go applies only to this larger category; it must not be promoted to the retained category.
