## 1. What this package is

This package is a compact scientific review bundle for the current state of the UQCF-GEM retained-coherence bridge.

It is designed to let an AI reviewer inspect, in one place:

1. the explicit mathematical spine of the bridge,
2. the status of each core equation or relation,
3. the first quantitative data-facing proxy confrontation,
4. the current context and novelty positioning,
5. and the hard falsification / scope boundaries of the present bridge version.

This is **not** a final theory manuscript, not a journal-ready cosmology paper, and not a full production-likelihood release. It is the current auditable scientific packet.

## 2. Why this packet exists

Earlier internal work established that the retained-coherence bridge had reached a meaningful internal threshold:

- certified local closure at baseline,
- empirical global non-branching across tested reduced-law families,
- a shell–microstate microscopic architecture,
- and robust self-auditing through scans and ablations.

However, AI review correctly identified three major weaknesses:

1. too much of the mathematics was trapped in code and prose rather than explicit equations,
2. the data-facing layer was qualitative rather than quantitatively confronted,
3. and the framework lacked a compact context / novelty positioning note.

This packet was assembled to address those three weaknesses directly.
