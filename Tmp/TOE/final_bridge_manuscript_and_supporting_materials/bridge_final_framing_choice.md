# Bridge Final Framing Choice

You now have two legitimate final framings.

## 1. Performance-first framing
Use this if you want:
- strongest metrics
- strongest robustness story
- strongest support for the current bridge packet

This is the safer and more evidence-dense publication route.

## 2. Elegance-first framing
Use this if you want:
- cleaner law language
- tighter conceptual unity
- a more compact final narrative

This is the more theory-forward route.

## Best recommendation

The strongest default choice is:

> **performance-first main paper, elegance-first discussion/conclusion**

That means:
- the main body uses the strongest hierarchical and calibrated results
- the discussion explains how the bridge can also be compressed toward a more elegant frontier meta-law

This gives both:
- empirical strength
- and conceptual ambition
