# Seam Status Note

## Purpose

This note consolidates the current evidence from the bridge-closure seam program and separates what is now strong enough to treat as certified seam behavior from what must still be treated as provisional.

The seam work was carried out after the hierarchical selector showed strong heldout and family-transfer performance but still required explicit local seam modeling.

---

## 1. Why this note is needed

The closure program no longer lives at the level of “does the regime map exist?”

It does.

The remaining question is whether the **transition bands** between the hardest local regimes are scientifically real, measurable, and stable enough to support a stronger bridge-closure claim.

Those local seams are:

1. the **weak ladder seam**
   - `weak_signal_multifrequency`
   - versus
   - `sub_boundary_weak_multifrequency`

2. the **boundary/crossover seam**
   - `transient_boundary_directional`
   - `switching_boundary_directional`
   - `weak_multifrequency_boundary_crossover`

This note records the current state of those seams.

---

## 2. Certified seam behavior

### A. The weak ladder seam is real
The weak ladder seam does not collapse directly into noise.
Local seam construction shows that the weak frontier contains at least two distinguishable ordered levels:

- `weak_signal_multifrequency`
- `sub_boundary_weak_multifrequency`

This means the lower weak ladder is real.

### B. The weak ladder seam is narrow
Dense seam construction shows that the weak seam exists, but occupies a relatively thin band.
Most synthetic seam cases are still pulled upward into `weak_signal_multifrequency`, while only a minority land in `sub_boundary_weak_multifrequency`.

So the seam is present, but currently biased upward.

### C. The boundary/crossover seam is soft and measurable
Dense seam construction shows that the boundary/crossover frontier does produce genuinely lower-margin cases.
This is the first clear evidence that the bridge is beginning to represent a real transition band rather than only hard forced partitions.

That makes the boundary/crossover seam scientifically meaningful.

---

## 3. Provisional seam behavior

### A. The crossover state is still provisional
The explicit state:

- `weak_multifrequency_boundary_crossover`

has proved useful as a local abstention or caution state in some selector tests.

However, in broader seam construction it does **not yet** emerge as a stable repeated bulk region.
The local band is dominated by:

- transient boundary structure
- switching boundary structure

rather than by a large repeated crossover interior.

So the crossover state should still be described as:
- useful
- local
- provisional

and not yet as a certified repeated regime.

### B. Weak seam thickness is not yet fully calibrated
The weak ladder seam exists, but the present models still compress much of the band upward into the stronger weak class.
That means the seam centerline is visible, but the thickness and full occupancy profile are not yet mature enough to claim exact closure.

---

## 4. Current best seam reading

The strongest current seam reading is:

- the **weak ladder seam** is real, ordered, and narrow
- the **boundary/crossover seam** is real and soft
- the **crossover state** remains useful but provisional
- the selector now models seam structure better than before, but the final seam law is not yet fully calibrated

That is the most honest current summary.

---

## 5. Strongest current admissible claim

The strongest current admissible claim is:

> the hierarchical retained-state bridge now supports measurable local seam structure, including a real weak ladder seam and a soft boundary/crossover transition band, although the explicit crossover state and exact seam thickness remain provisional.

That is the correct current claim level.

---

## 6. Strongest inadmissible overclaim

It is not yet justified to claim:

- that seam thickness is fully calibrated
- that the crossover state is already a broadly repeated regime
- that all local transition bands are finalized
- or that the seam audit is fully complete

Those remain open.

---

## 7. Best current publication posture

For a public or semi-public progress note, the seam results should be described this way:

- weak ladder seam: **supported**
- boundary/crossover band: **supported**
- crossover state: **provisional**
- final seam calibration: **ongoing**

That is both strong and honest.

---

## 8. Best next step

The strongest next move is:

> proceed to the **transfer audit**, while carrying forward the current seam status exactly as:
- certified where repeated
- provisional where still local or thin

That is the right next stage of bridge closure.
