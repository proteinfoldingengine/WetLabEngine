# Round 16 Results Explained

## What was tested
Round 16 asked whether the **same frozen scaffold** could survive a broader cluster sample without falling apart, once the test set expanded beyond the initial Round 15 checkpoint.

That is why the main result is not “all positive.”  
The main result is **bounded behavior**.

## Why the mix of positive and neutral is good
A fake-looking round would often have every case come back glowing. This one did not.

- Some systems were strongly positive.
- Some systems stayed neutral.
- A harder second look on Abell 2744 reduced the strength of the claim instead of inflating it.

That reads more like an actual stress test than a hype cycle.

## The most important non-event
**No catastrophic failures happened.**

A round can survive neutrals.
A round can survive source misses.
A round can survive proxy-stage caveats.

What usually kills a round is obvious breakdown.

That never appeared here.

## The real takeaway
The scaffold expanded and stayed bounded.

That is the result.
