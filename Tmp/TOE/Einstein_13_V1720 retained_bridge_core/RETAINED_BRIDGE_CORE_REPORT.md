## 1. The primitives (what is assumed)

The entire model is generated by one operator and two structural properties.

**The recombination kernel:**
```
T(dx; q, g) = dx + g · ( roll(dx) ⊙ q − dx ⊙ roll(q) )
op(x, y; g)  = x + y + g · ( roll(x) ⊙ y − x ⊙ roll(y) )
```
where `roll` is the unit cyclic shift, `⊙` is elementwise product, `g` the coupling (operating
value g = 0.17). Nothing else is assumed: no metric, no time, no space, no Hilbert space.

**Two primitive properties of the kernel** (everything else is derived from these):
- **P1 — Non-associativity / order-sensitivity.** The grouping and order of recombination change
  the result. Source of all geometric and interference structure.
- **P2 — Forward-only pruning / non-invertibility.** Recombination retains forward and cannot be
  un-done. Source of all non-unitary and time-directional structure.

The control that isolates P1 is the **associative kernel** (`op = x + y`, or linear shift). The
control that isolates P2 is the **invertible transport** (skew part only, `exp(g·½(J−Jᵀ))`,
exactly norm-preserving). These two controls are the master nulls of the whole program.

## 3. Established results (each independently controlled)

### Geometry
- **Local metric is real and Riemannian.** Positive-definite, anisotropic, triangle-inequality
  exact.
- **The native connection is non-metric, and non-metricity is the PRIMARY effect.** `Q ∝ g`
  (linear, leading order); curvature `R ∝ g²` (quadratic, secondary). Non-metricity is
  **shear-dominated** (traceless ≈ 3× the Weyl/trace part), stable across coupling.
- **Curvature is real, positive, isotropic.** h-stable (identical across finite-difference steps
  1e-3…3e-5); flat-metric control gives exactly 0; sphere control gives the known value (validated
  scheme). Uniformly positive, grows with radius.
- **All geometric effects are sourced by P1 (non-associativity).** Every quantity vanishes at
  g = 0 and the associative control is flat at all g.

### Globalization
- **The geometry does not globalize.** Local metrics do not glue across charts (mismatch = random
  patchwork). Under independent averaging the non-metricity coheres (does not cancel). Under
  coupled renormalization-group coarse-graining the non-metricity is a **scale-invariant fixed
  point in both magnitude (flow ratio 1.002) and shape (shear/Weyl ≈ 2.9 at all scales)**. No
  effective metric sector emerges at any scale.

### Interference and dynamics
- **Amplitude-like history interference is real.** Alternative recombination histories between the
  same endpoints partially cancel: `‖Σhᵢ‖/Σ‖hᵢ‖ < 1`, monotonic in g (0% at g=0 → 33% at g=0.6),
  exactly 1.000 (no cancellation) in the associative and flat controls. Sourced by P1.
- **The interference is non-unitary / dissipative.** No norm conservation (1.019, scattered);
  generator symmetric-dominated (skew fraction 0.37); not unitarily representable (20% error).
  Consistent with P2 (non-invertibility precludes unitarity).
- **The non-unitarity is an arrow of time — dissipative/statistical, not strict Lyapunov.**
  Pruning-sourced (every signal collapses to 1.000 ± 0.000 in the invertible control); irreversible
  (closed loops drift; reverse does not undo forward); directional in the ensemble (mean trajectory
  rises 93% of steps) and cumulative (distance-from-origin nets 0.09→2.46). NOT a strict scalar
  Lyapunov function: per-trajectory monotonicity is 50–62% (near random walk with weak drift); no
  state-defined scalar is strictly monotone (V1719).

### Thermodynamics (new arc, first test)
- **The arrow does NOT obey a thermodynamic entropy-production law (T1 closed-negative).** State
  entropy change is pruning-sourced (zero in invertible control) and g-scaling, but it is not
  sign-definite per step (frac σ>0 ≈ 0.47) and the associative control produces a *stronger,
  opposite-signed* change — so the entropy change is generic transport-reshaping, not
  non-associativity-specific entropy production. All three pre-registered state entropies (support,
  participation, reconstruction) fail identically. T2 (fluctuation theorem) and T3 (effective
  temperature) were NOT run, because they require a legitimate entropy/work functional that T1 did
  not yield (running them without one would be fabrication).
