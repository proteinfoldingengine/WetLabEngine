### sub_boundary_weak_multifrequency
This state means:

- some recurring multifrequency evidence is present

It is best interpreted as:

> a **weak-channel intermediate band** below weak multifrequency success and above noisy contrast.

That is why the word **sub-boundary** remains appropriate.
