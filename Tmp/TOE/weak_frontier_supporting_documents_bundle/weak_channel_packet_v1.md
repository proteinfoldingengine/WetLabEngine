# Weak-Channel Packet v1

## Purpose

This packet formalizes the current weak-channel refinement inside the vector-family selector.

It records the emerging weak-channel ladder:

1. **weak_signal_multifrequency**
2. **sub_boundary_weak_multifrequency**
3. **weak_multifrequency_boundary_crossover**
4. **noisy_scalar_like**

This is now the clearest scientific structure for the weakest vector-side seam.

---

## 1. Why this packet is now justified

The weak seam was originally a single unresolved problem:

- ultra-weak multifrequency
vs
- noisy contrast

Refinement work showed that this seam does not collapse into a simple two-state split.

Instead, the weak-channel region now separates into multiple distinct levels.

This was supported by:
- repeated weak-channel detector work
- seam-family generation
- sub-boundary classification
- and the tightened crossover detector

That is enough to justify a formal weak-channel packet.

---

## 2. Weak-channel ladder

### A. weak_signal_multifrequency
Meaning:
- the case is weak, but still has enough recurring multifrequency structure
- weak-channel evidence is strong enough for positive weak multifrequency assignment

### B. sub_boundary_weak_multifrequency
Meaning:
- the case shows recurring weak multifrequency evidence
- stronger than plain noisy contrast
- weaker than true weak multifrequency success
- best interpreted as an intermediate sub-boundary state

### C. weak_multifrequency_boundary_crossover
Meaning:
- the case carries evidence from both:
  - weak multifrequency
  - balanced transient boundary
- the selector should abstain rather than force a brittle choice

### D. noisy_scalar_like
Meaning:
- residual geometry is too degraded or too unstable
- not enough structured evidence remains for weak multifrequency assignment

---

## 3. Why the crossover state matters

The crossover state was introduced because the selector reached a clear tradeoff pair:

- `exotic_weak_multifreq_a`
- `balanced_blur`

A broad crossover detector was too aggressive.
A tightened detector successfully marked the balanced weak-boundary case as crossover without stealing the true weak multifrequency case.

That makes the crossover state scientifically meaningful.
