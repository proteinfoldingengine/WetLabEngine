# Public Progress Note — Memory-Lift Regime Map and Weak Frontier Status

## Working title
**A Structured Regime Map for Memory-Lift Dynamics: Scalar, Vector, and Weak-Channel Refinements**

## Public-facing summary

We have been studying whether different residual/failure geometries call for different memory geometries rather than one universal correction model.

At this point, the strongest supportable result is not a final universal law. It is a structured regime map.

### What now appears established

The current work supports a broad split between:

- **scalar-side regimes**
- **vector-side regimes**
- and meaningful **boundary structure** between them

On the scalar side, the current evidence supports:
- **scalar_positive**
- **scalar_restricted**
- **boundary_scalar_side**

On the vector side, the evidence supports that directional regimes are not all the same. The vector family now appears internally structured, including:

- **coherent_rotational_directional**
- **multifrequency_directional**
- **weak_signal_multifrequency**
- **sub_boundary_weak_multifrequency**
- **noisy_scalar_like**

We also use a provisional abstention-style state for especially delicate crossover cases:
- **weak_multifrequency_boundary_crossover**

### Strongest current result

One of the strongest repeated findings is that the vector side is not one bucket.

In the tested families:
- **rotational directional** cases repeatedly favored a higher-retention vector-memory law
- **multifrequency directional** cases repeatedly favored a lower-retention vector-memory law

That split repeated across multiple designed and fresh-family tests and remains one of the clearest structured results in the program.

### What was learned on the weak frontier

The weakest vector-side seam originally looked like a blur between:
- ultra-weak multifrequency structure
- and noisy contrast

After repeated refinement, the current evidence suggests a more honest ladder:

1. **weak_signal_multifrequency**
2. **sub_boundary_weak_multifrequency**
3. **noisy_scalar_like**

That lower weak-channel ladder now appears to repeat across broader seam-focused sweeps.

The more explicit crossover state remains useful as a conservative abstention mechanism, but it is **not yet** established as a broadly repeated regime in the same way.

## What we are not claiming

We are **not** claiming:
- a final universal selector
- a completed theory of all regime boundaries
- or a publication-ready proof that every weak-channel state now generalizes cleanly across all future out-of-suite families

The weakest seam remains the least mature part of the map.

## Best current scientific framing

The best current summary is:

> The model now supports a real multi-channel regime structure in which scalar, vector, and boundary behaviors separate meaningfully, and the vector side is internally stratified enough to require its own family map and selector logic. The lower weak frontier now shows repeatable structure, while the top crossover seam remains provisional.

## Why this matters

The importance of the result is methodological as much as architectural.

Instead of forcing all difficult cases into one generic failure bucket, the work suggests that:
- different residual geometries may require different memory geometries
- internal sub-branches can emerge inside a broader family
- and the weakest seams may be more honestly represented by intermediate or abstaining states rather than by forced binary assignments

That is a stronger and more falsifiable position than claiming a single universal correction rule too early.

## Current publication recommendation

This work is ready for:
- an internal research memorandum
- a cautious public progress note
- or a technical writeup framed as **structured intermediate results**

It is **not yet** best framed as a final public claim that the full weak frontier is solved.

## Suggested short public version

We are seeing evidence that memory-lift dynamics are not one undifferentiated problem. A structured regime map is emerging.

The scalar side appears real but bounded. The vector side appears real and internally split. And the weakest frontier does not collapse directly from weak multifrequency into noise — it now shows a repeatable lower ladder with a still-provisional crossover seam.

That is where the science stands today.
