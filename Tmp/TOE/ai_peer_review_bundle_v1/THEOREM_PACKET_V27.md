## 8. Official downstream affine corollary family

The downstream Brier-sign layer is frozen as an official affine corollary family.

### Family form
Use:

`(2a - 1) * Delta_Var + 2b - Delta_Bias2`

with residual tolerance `|r|`.

A point is certified positive when:

`(2a - 1) * Delta_Var + 2b - Delta_Bias2 - |r| > 0`

A point is certified negative when:

`(2a - 1) * Delta_Var + 2b - Delta_Bias2 + |r| < 0`

### Why it is a family
Coverage and precision trade off continuously, so the downstream object is a precision–coverage operating family rather than a single tuned rule.

### Operating interpretation
- **high-coverage** = hard operational default
- **balanced** = softer structure-preserving alternative
- **high-confidence** = stricter alternative

This is the cleanest downstream restricted corollary layer.
