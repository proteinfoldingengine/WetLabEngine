# Interaction-Selected Routing in a Small Retained Source-Current Network

## Abstract

We present a small finite-network simulation demonstrating that bare source anchoring and conservation do not canonically determine a unique retained routing map Phi. In a directed graph with nontrivial cycle space, the source-current equation admits an affine family of conserved currents, J=J0+Za, where Z spans the kernel of the boundary/incidence operator. This nonuniqueness appears in a small graph, showing that the obstruction is algebraic/topological rather than a scale or compute artifact. We further show that network-only Hodge/minimum-action selection does not close Phi unless the metric W is itself canonically derived; different plausible local positive metrics select different conserved currents. By contrast, an admissible response operator closes the ambiguity exactly when it resolves the cycle space, rank(RZ)=dim(Z). The result supports an interaction-selected Phi law within the retained source-current framework while leaving raw Genesis-Hodge closure and ADM/GR correspondence open.

## Claim Boundary

This simulation supports a finite graph identifiability result. It does not derive physical spacetime, ADM, or general relativity.
