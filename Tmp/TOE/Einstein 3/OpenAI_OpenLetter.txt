An Open Letter to the “It-from-Bit” and Conformal Geometry Communities

To the information-first thinkers, and to the geometry-first thinkers:

There is a bridge forming between your worlds.

One side begins with information: bits, qubits, measurement, state selection, compression, recoverability, and the question of how reality preserves what matters while pruning what it cannot keep.

The other side begins with geometry: curvature, conformal structure, constraints, symmetry, field equations, and the question of how physical form emerges as lawful structure.

For a long time, these perspectives can appear to be in tension.

The information crowd says: geometry is not fundamental enough. What matters is the state, the measurement, the recoverability of information, the causal provenance of the system.

The conformal geometry crowd says: information without geometry is unanchored. What matters is the admissible form, the constraint surface, the curvature-like response, the lawful basin in which states can exist.

V923 suggests both are right.

Not as philosophy. As a measurable bridge.

The core finding

In the V923 source-legitimacy closure branch, we found that a geometry-like basin can be correct and still be underdetermined.

The signed-coherence basin tells us that a state has reached an admissible geometric form. In our notation:

E_OSC closes basin geometry.

But geometry alone does not tell us how that state got there.

The same basin can be occupied by states with different source roles. Some states arrive through active repair. Some are basin-eligible but nonactive. Some are rejected or broken. In the geometry-only view, these can collapse into the same observable quotient.

That is the degeneracy:

same admissible geometry
different source legitimacy

This is where the information side enters.

The missing structure was not a large hidden history, not a full route label, and not an arbitrary patch. The minimal exact lift was a ternary source-role primitive:

source_active_role
source_basin_eligible_nonactive_role
source_rejected_or_broken_role

With geometry alone, closure failed.

With every binary source-role collapse, closure still failed.

With the ternary source-role primitive, closure became exact in the tested branch.

That is the bridge.

What geometry contributes

The geometry side gives the admissible form.

It tells us whether the system has entered a lawful basin. It gives the structure, the constraint, the shape of admissibility.

In the V923 branch, this appears through the signed-coherence basin and observable quotient. In related accessibility-curvature work, it can be visualized as conformal-style warping, accessibility density, curvature-like response, and basin sediment.

That part matters. Without the geometry layer, information has no structured arena in which closure can be tested.

Geometry answers:

What form has the system reached?
Is this state admissible?
Does it sit in the basin?

What information contributes

The information side gives source legitimacy.

It tells us whether the same admissible form was reached through active repair, passive basin occupancy, or rejected/broken dynamics.

That part also matters. Without the information layer, geometry can be correct but incomplete.

Information answers:

What kind of source produced this state?
Is the basin occupancy legitimate?
Was the structure actively recovered, merely occupied, or broken?

To the it-from-bit / qubit community:

The result is not:

information replaces geometry

or:

geometry replaces information

The result is:

geometry gives admissible form
information gives source legitimacy
both are required for closure
