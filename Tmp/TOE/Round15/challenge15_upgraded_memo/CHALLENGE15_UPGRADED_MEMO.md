Round 15 is materially harder than the galaxy validation rounds. The source material is heterogeneous, machine-unfriendly, and often requires page-level locking before any scoring can even begin. Several early candidate sources turned out to be general reviews or irrelevant papers, which slowed the first phase of progress.

Despite that, the current checkpoint shows:
- one relaxed cluster yielding a positive prototype,
- one relaxed cluster yielding a neutral but bounded prototype,
- and the canonical merger-offset test yielding a positive prototype offset signal.

That combination is much harder to dismiss than a single isolated win.
