# Round 12 Falsification Conditions

Round 12 is counted as failed if any of the following occur:

## Primary failure conditions
1. Positive improvement rate < 70%
2. Mean RMSE improvement < 5 km/s
3. Catastrophic failures > 2

## Reproducibility failure conditions
4. The packet cannot be run from the published code and inputs without hidden intervention
5. The operator requires per-galaxy retuning to meet threshold
6. The published scoring protocol differs from the executed scoring protocol

## Interpretation failure condition
7. Claimed regime sensitivity or failure localization cannot be reproduced from the emitted results

## Important note
A partial success is allowed scientifically, but it does not count as a Round 12 win under the locked challenge definition.
