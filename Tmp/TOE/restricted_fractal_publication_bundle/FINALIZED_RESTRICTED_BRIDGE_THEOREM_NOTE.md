\[

- \(\lambda r\) is the residual-directed coarse correction
- \(\xi\) is the residual-orthogonal innovation

\[

\[
b_0 = \frac{\lambda_0^2V_0(C_0+V_0)}{2(C_0+\lambda_0V_0)}.

\[
\Delta \mathrm{Cov}=a_0\,\Delta \mathrm{Var}+b_0+\epsilon

with \(\epsilon\) controlled by:
- gain drift
- curvature
- innovation scale

This is the base theorem already established in restricted form.
